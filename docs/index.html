Combat Design

Every system talks to every other system

Attacks broadcast trajectories. The dodge reads them in real time. Fatigue punishes panic. The weapon shifts to match the moment.

⚔

Context-Gated Dodge

Dodge only fires when you're inside an attack's alert box. No threat nearby? The input is ignored. No spam, no panic rolls — just reads.

➚

Attack Trajectory Chains

Every projectile and melee swing defines its path as ordered waypoints. The dodge system reads the current segment direction and time-to-arrival in real time.

⏱

Fatigue System

Each dodge costs stamina. I-frame duration shrinks, dodge distance drops, cooldown grows. Five rapid dodges? You're exhausted and locked out.

⚖

Shape-Shifting Weapon

The Meta Morph Blade shifts between pike, long sword, dual short swords, and scythe — each with different combat roles and risk profiles.

⚫

Full VR Body

Not floating hands. Full-body IK driven by your headset — crouching, crawling, foot planting, spine lean. You have a body and it moves with you.

🌐

Planetoid Combat

The entire combat system works in actor-local space. Dodge on any surface, any gravity direction. The tangent plane is always correct.

The Dodge System

A dodge system that reads the fight

Your stick sets the direction. The system helps — but never overrides you.

Attack Spawns → Trajectory Chain → Alert Box Active
Player Overlaps → Jump + Stick Flick → Dodge Accepted
Stick Direction + Light Assist = Final Direction
Fatigue++ → I-Frames ↓ → Distance ↓ → Cooldown ↑

Context gate — no empty dodges

Each attack carries an alert box — an overlap sphere sized by the developer. Dodge input is rejected unless you're inside one. Fast projectiles get wide alert boxes. Tight melee swings get small ones.

Assist that never fights you

Your stick chooses the direction. The system applies a perpendicular nudge — never opposing your input. If you dodge into the attack, the system pushes you under or over it with a duck phase while i-frames cover the pass-through.

Live tracking, decaying influence

Direction isn't locked at start. The dodge re-evaluates every tick with decaying assist weight, so it reacts to moving attacks while still committing to your input by the end.

Fatigue punishes panic

Every dodge increments a hidden fatigue counter. As fatigue rises, i-frame duration shrinks toward 30% of base, dodge distance drops to half, and cooldown doubles.

Hit max fatigue and you're locked out entirely — no dodges for a full second. Fatigue decays after 1.5s of not dodging.

The message: you can dodge through anything, but not everything. Read the attacks. Pick your moments. Panic costs you.

VR Presence

You have a body. It moves with you.

Full-body inverse kinematics driven by your headset and controllers in real time.

HMD HEIGHT → BODY IK
Standing Full locomotion animation · light IK
Crouching Pelvis drops · spine leans · legs IK-bend
Crawling Full crawl animation takeover
Arms Controller → IK hand & elbow targets
Feet Planted to ground · knee targets from calf bones

Three pose states, zero jitter

Your real-world height maps to standing, crouching, or crawling with hysteresis to prevent flickering at boundaries. The system reads ceiling heights from the environment to gate transitions.

Proportional IK, never full override

Light crouch = light IK with visible leg animation. Deep crouch = strong IK for floor contact. Leg animation is never fully replaced — the character always looks alive.

Arms track your controllers

Motion controller transforms are converted into skeletal mesh component space. Elbow joint targets are computed to bend naturally — backward and outward.

The Swahili Realm

Floating planetoids. Local gravity. Every surface is a floor.

An Anomaly Realm — a three-dimensional scattering of floating landmasses with gravitational pulls that shift between and around them.

🌎

Actor-Space Everything

No world-up assumption anywhere. Dodge projects to the tangent plane. Body rotation uses local up. Wall running scores against any surface orientation. The code doesn't care which way is "down."

⚡

Mid-Tier Low Universal Energy

Magic is the primary combat tool. Raw energy won't save you. Deep knowledge and clever application beat raw power — which is exactly where a 17-year-old with 60 years of half-remembered study thrives.

⚙

Mecha Suits

Summonable mechanical constructs that run without magic after deployment. Sturdy builds survive multiple realms. Powerful builds crumble outside their tier. The tradeoff is strategic, not numerical.
