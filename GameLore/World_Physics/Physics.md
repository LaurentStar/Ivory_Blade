# Physics

> *Each realm writes its own rules. Gravity, space, time, matter -- none of it is guaranteed to work the way you expect.*

---

## Baseline Concept

Each realm in the Mirralata System can have its own laws of physics. There is no single set of universal physical rules that applies everywhere. What gravity does, how space behaves, how matter interacts -- all of this can vary from realm to realm.

Some realms follow physics that are recognizable to a being from any standard world. Others diverge in small ways -- stronger gravity, different atmospheric composition, unusual material properties. And some are Anomaly Realms, where physics break down entirely into impossible logic.

---

## How Physics Varies

### Standard Realm Variations

Standard realms maintain internally consistent physics, even if those physics differ from other realms. A being entering a standard realm can learn the rules and rely on them. Examples of standard variation:

- **Gravity strength and direction** -- Stronger, weaker, or pulling in unexpected directions. Consistent within the realm.
- **Atmospheric and environmental conditions** -- Different gases, pressures, temperatures, light spectra. Challenging but learnable.
- **Material properties** -- Metals that behave differently, liquids with unusual viscosity, surfaces with unexpected friction. Requires adaptation but follows patterns.
- **Spatial geometry** -- Standard realms maintain Euclidean or near-Euclidean space. Distances work as expected. Maps are reliable.

The key trait of standard realm physics: they can be studied, predicted, and mastered. A being who spends enough time in a standard realm will learn how it works and can fight, build, and travel effectively within its rules.

### Anomaly Realm Physics

Anomaly Realms do not maintain internal consistency. Their physics are broken, contradictory, or impossible by the standards of any normal realm.

- **Gravity may shift without warning** -- pulling in different directions on different sides of the same structure, reversing mid-step, or applying selectively to certain objects
- **Space may contradict itself** -- a hallway longer in one direction than the other, rooms that don't exist when you turn around, distances that change depending on when you measure them
- **Cause and effect may decouple** -- an action produces one result the first time and a different result the second time under identical conditions
- **Impossible structures** -- architecture or terrain that could not exist under any consistent physics, yet does

Anomaly Realm physics cannot be fully studied or mastered. A being can learn tendencies and develop instincts, but they cannot build a reliable model of how the realm behaves because the realm does not follow its own rules consistently.

## The Swahili Realm -- Physics Profile

The Swahili Realm is an Anomaly Realm. Its physics are defined by two primary characteristics:

### Planetoid Structure

The Swahili Realm is not a conventional landscape. It is composed of **planetoids** -- small planetary bodies, floating landmasses, or fragmented terrain distributed through the realm's space. There is no single ground, no single sky. The environment is a three-dimensional scattering of surfaces at various scales.

This means:
- Travel is not linear. Moving from one location to another may require crossing gaps between planetoids.
- Structures, settlements, and ecosystems exist on individual planetoids or clusters of connected ones.
- Line of sight, acoustics, and spatial awareness operate differently than on a single continuous surface.

### Strange Gravity

Gravity in the Swahili Realm does not follow standard rules. The nature of the strangeness includes:

- Gravitational pull may orient toward individual planetoids rather than a single directional "down"
- Gravity may vary in strength across different parts of the realm
- Transitional zones between planetoids may have reduced, reversed, or competing gravitational pulls
- The protagonist's wall running and agile movement mechanics are a direct response to this environment -- in a realm where gravity cannot be taken for granted, the ability to run along surfaces at any orientation is not a trick but a survival skill

### What Works Normally

Despite the anomalous gravity and spatial structure, the Swahili Realm maintains semi-normal behavior in other areas:

- **Magic functions** at a level consistent with its mid-tier low Universal Energy
- **Materials behave predictably** -- swords cut, armor stops blows, structures have expected strength
- **Beings remain biologically consistent** -- bodies work as expected, injuries heal normally, physical training holds

The anomaly is primarily in gravity and spatial structure, not in the fundamental behavior of matter or biology.

## Implications for Combat and Traversal

Physics variation fundamentally changes how beings fight and move. In the Swahili Realm specifically:

- **Vertical and horizontal are relative.** A fight can move across surfaces at any orientation. An enemy on the "ceiling" of a planetoid is standing on their own ground.
- **Falling is not always dangerous -- but losing orientation is.** In transitional gravity zones, a fall might curve, slow, or redirect. Getting lost between gravitational fields is the real threat.
- **Ranged attacks behave unpredictably.** Projectiles (magical and physical) are subject to the same gravitational inconsistencies. A straight shot may curve. A thrown weapon may not land where expected.
- **The pike makes sense here.** A reach weapon with leverage and momentum is valuable in an environment where closing distance may require crossing gravity boundaries. The pike allows the protagonist to engage before committing to uncertain terrain.
