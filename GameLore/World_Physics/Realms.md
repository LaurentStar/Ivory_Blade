# Realms and the Mirralata System

> *Each realm is a bubble containing an entire universe. The protagonist is flying through them in a spaceship, heading for the center.*

---

## The Mirralata System

The Mirralata System is the totality of existence in Ivory Blade's world. It is not a single planet, continent, or galaxy. It is a structure composed of realms -- discrete, self-contained universes arranged in a system that has a center.

The protagonist's journey is to reach that center.

---

## What Realms Are

Each realm is like a **bubble containing an entire universe**. A realm is not a small pocket dimension or a single room. It is a complete reality with its own space, its own environments, its own inhabitants, and its own rules.

Every realm is governed by the three pillars -- Universal Energy, magic, and physics -- but the specific values and behaviors of each pillar vary from realm to realm. One realm might have high Universal Energy and standard physics. Another might have low Universal Energy and anomalous gravity. A third might be a full Anomaly Realm where nothing follows consistent rules.

Realms are separated from one another. You cannot walk from one realm to the next. Crossing requires a vessel.

---

## Travel Between Realms

### The Spaceship

Beings travel between realms in **flying spaceships**. This is the primary method of inter-realm transit. The spaceship is not a metaphor -- it is a physical vessel that crosses the boundaries between realm-bubbles and delivers its passengers into the next reality.

The protagonist travels with Captain Ys and his crew aboard such a vessel.

### The Apotheosis Track

The Apotheosis Track is a specific route or pathway within the Mirralata System. It appears to be a structured path leading toward the system's center, accessible through a special door.

Captain Ys and his crew navigate the protagonist past surrounding conflicts to reach the Mirralata System's special door to the Apotheosis Track. This door deposits them into the Swahili Realm.

Whether the Apotheosis Track is the only route to the center, one of many routes, or a specific corridor that passes through particular realms in sequence remains to be determined.

## Realm Types

### Standard Realms

Standard realms have internally consistent rules for all three pillars. Their physics, while potentially different from other realms, can be studied, learned, and predicted. A being who spends time in a standard realm can build a reliable understanding of how it works.

Standard realms vary in Universal Energy level, which determines the combat dynamics within them (see [UniversalEnergy.md](UniversalEnergy.md) and [Magic.md](Magic.md)).

### Anomaly Realms

Anomaly Realms operate on impossible logic. Their physics may be internally inconsistent, contradictory, or broken. They can still have measurable Universal Energy levels and support magic -- the anomaly is in how the realm behaves physically, not necessarily in the absence of the three pillars.

Anomaly Realms may contain entities (ghosts) that do not exist under normal realm rules -- artifacts of broken logic, presences that the impossible physics allows to persist.

The Swahili Realm, where the game takes place, is an Anomaly Realm with mid-tier low Universal Energy, functional magic, and anomalous gravity/planetoid structure.

See [Physics.md](Physics.md) for detailed Anomaly Realm physics.

## Cross-Realm Power Dynamics

The realm a being comes from shapes how they perform in other realms. This is not a matter of personal power alone -- it is a consequence of the environment that built them.

### Descending (Higher to Lower UE)

Beings from high-UE realms entering lower-UE realms lose access to the energy that sustained their power. Some cannot survive the transition at all -- their bodies tear apart without the Universal Energy that maintained their structural integrity. The more powerful and UE-dependent a being, the more vulnerable they are to descent.

### Ascending (Lower to Higher UE)

Beings from low-UE realms entering higher-UE realms gain a massive power increase. Surviving in a low-energy environment requires baseline toughness that high-realm beings never needed to develop. When that toughness meets abundant energy, the result is disproportionate power.

### The Light Threshold (Ascending Too Far)

If the gap between a being's origin realm and the destination realm is too extreme, the being does not simply become powerful -- they **turn into light**. Their form dissolves into the realm's energy. They become a universe within the realm, omnipresent and godlike but without a body.

This is functionally death, though it is calm rather than violent. Exceptionally composed individuals can maintain consciousness in this state and some have managed to retreat back into a lower realm to reconstitute. But most are simply absorbed.

The Mirralata System punishes beings who cross too far from their native energy level in either direction: descend too far and you tear apart; ascend too far and you dissolve into light.

This asymmetry means that the direction of travel through the Mirralata System fundamentally changes what the protagonist can do and what threatens her. See [UniversalEnergy.md](UniversalEnergy.md) for full details.

## The Swahili Realm -- The Game's Setting

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Anomaly Realm |
| **Universal Energy** | Mid-tier low |
| **Magic** | Functional -- protagonist's primary combat tool |
| **Physics** | Anomalous -- planetoid structure, strange gravity |
| **Current State** | Active war between two groups (TBD) |

The Swahili Realm is where the game takes place. The protagonist arrives via the Apotheosis Track with Captain Ys and his crew. She must navigate the realm's anomalous terrain and an active war while pursuing her goal of reaching the center of the Mirralata System to restore Kim Tiernan's soul.

## Open Questions

1. **Realm arrangement** -- Are realms arranged in concentric layers (outer to inner), a branching tree, a web, or something else? Does "moving toward the center" follow a linear path or require navigating a complex structure?
2. **How many realms exist?** Dozens? Hundreds? Infinite? Does anyone have a complete map?
3. **What is at the center?** The highest Universal Energy? The lowest? Something that transcends the three-pillar system entirely? What does the protagonist believe is there, and is she right?
4. **Realm boundaries** -- What exists between realms? Void? Dimensional barriers? Is the space between realms traversable, or do ships jump directly from one bubble to the next?
5. **The Apotheosis Track** -- Is it a physical road, a dimensional corridor, a series of connected realm doors? Who built it? Is it maintained, abandoned, or contested?
6. **Can realms be destroyed?** What happens to a realm's inhabitants if the bubble collapses?
7. **Realm governance** -- Do individual realms have their own civilizations and governments independent of one another? Is there any inter-realm political structure?
8. **The protagonist's home realm** -- What is the name and UE level of the realm containing the elf-controlled nation where she grew up?
