# Universal Energy

> *The raw fuel of existence. The higher the Universal Energy, the more powerful a being can become -- and the more dependent on it they are.*

---

## What It Is

Universal Energy is the ability to generate or gather power from the universe itself. It is not magic. It is not physical strength. It is a separate, fundamental force that permeates every realm in the Mirralata System at varying levels.

Universal Energy determines the ceiling for how powerful a being can become within a given realm. In high-UE realms, that ceiling is astronomically high. In low-UE realms, it barely exists.

---

## How It Scales

### High Universal Energy Realms

Beings can become extraordinarily powerful. At the upper extremes, individuals can reach speed, durability, and destructive output comparable to Dragon Ball-level characters. The laws of what a body can physically achieve are rewritten by the sheer density of available energy. Flight, planet-shaking strikes, near-instantaneous movement -- all possible when Universal Energy is abundant enough.

In these realms, magic is pointless for combat. Universal Energy so thoroughly outclasses magical output that casting a spell in a fight is like throwing a rock at someone who can shatter continents with a punch.

### Mid-Level Realms

Universal Energy is present but not dominant. Beings receive meaningful enhancement -- increased speed, durability, strength -- but not at the extreme end of the scale. A combatant supplemented by mid-level UE is significantly more capable than a baseline human, but not invincible.

At this level, magic becomes the primary combat tool and Universal Energy serves as a supplement.

### Low Universal Energy Realms

Beings are limited. A person in a low-UE realm becomes more like a regular human regardless of their training, innate power, or potential. The energy simply is not there to fuel extraordinary abilities.

Magic becomes difficult to cast. Physical baseline -- strength, reflexes, endurance, wits -- becomes critical. This is where the body you actually built matters, not the power you can channel.

### Near-Zero Realms

Everything depends on what you are without Universal Energy. No enhancement. No easy magic. Whatever you brought with you in your muscles, your bones, and your mind is all you have.

## Cross-Realm Travel Effects

Universal Energy creates an asymmetric power dynamic between beings from different realm levels.

### Higher-Level Beings Entering Lower-Level Realms

Some creatures physically cannot survive in lower Universal Energy realms. Their bodies depend on Universal Energy to maintain structural integrity -- their cells, their organs, their very existence is sustained by ambient energy. Remove that energy, and they tear apart.

The more powerful a being has become through Universal Energy, the more dependent on it they may be. A warrior who has spent centuries absorbing and channeling high-level UE has fundamentally altered their biology to require it. Dropping them into a low-UE realm is like removing the atmosphere from a room -- they suffocate at a cellular level.

This means some of the most powerful beings in the Mirralata System are effectively prisoners of their own realm level. They cannot descend.

### Lower-Level Beings Entering Higher-Level Realms

They gain a massive power increase. This is not a paradox -- it is a consequence of survival pressure.

Surviving in a low-Universal-Energy realm requires a being to be fundamentally tougher at baseline. Where a high-level being can rely on abundant UE to sustain and enhance them, a low-level being must be built to function without it. Their bodies are harder, their biology more self-sufficient, their baseline capabilities higher out of necessity.

When that baseline -- already optimized for survival in harsh, energy-poor conditions -- is suddenly flooded with Universal Energy, the result is disproportionate power. A low-realm warrior entering a high-UE realm gains enhancement on top of an already formidable foundation. They punch above their expected weight class because the denominator they started from was already high.

### The Light Threshold -- When the Gap Is Too Large

If a being from too low a realm enters too high a realm, the power gain does not cap out at "extremely strong." It keeps going until the being **turns into light**.

When the gap between a being's origin level and the destination realm's Universal Energy is too extreme, the flood of energy does not simply enhance the being -- it dissolves them. Their physical form cannot contain the energy pouring into it. They expand, diffuse, and become light. They become a universe within the realm -- omnipresent, godlike, everywhere at once.

This is functionally death. The being loses their body, their form, their ability to act as an individual. They become part of the realm's fabric.

However, it is not violent. It is described as **calm and relaxed**. The dissolution is not painful. It is a quiet unraveling.

**Maintaining consciousness:** Very calm and relaxed individuals can maintain consciousness even after becoming light. They exist as a diffused presence -- aware, thinking, experiencing -- but without form. Some who have maintained enough self-awareness have managed to **retreat back into a lower realm**, reconstituting themselves by moving to an environment where the energy level no longer overwhelms their structure.

This means the Light Threshold is not necessarily permanent, but recovery requires extraordinary composure. Most beings who cross the threshold are simply gone -- absorbed into the higher realm as ambient energy. Only those with the mental discipline to remain themselves in a state of total dissolution have any chance of returning.

**The symmetry of cross-realm danger:**
- Too high a being descending too low: **tears apart** (violent, structural failure)
- Too low a being ascending too high: **becomes light** (calm, dissolution into the realm)

Both are fatal without extraordinary circumstances. The Mirralata System punishes beings who cross too far from their native energy level in either direction.

## Implications for the Protagonist

The protagonist comes from an elf-controlled nation (realm level TBD, likely mid-range). The game takes place in the Swahili Realm, which has mid-tier low Universal Energy.

In the Swahili Realm, she can access a little bit of UE -- enough to supplement her abilities modestly, but not enough to transform her into something superhuman. She remains largely bound by her physical body's real capabilities, enhanced slightly by available energy.

If she were to enter a higher-UE realm, her baseline toughness (trained in conditions with limited energy) would translate into significant power gains. If she descended to a lower realm, she would lose even the modest UE supplement she currently has.
