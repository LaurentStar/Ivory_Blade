# Magic

> *In the highest realms, magic is irrelevant. In the lowest, it is impossible. In between is where it matters -- and where the protagonist was built to fight.*

---

## Magic and Universal Energy -- The Inverse Relationship

Magic and Universal Energy are separate forces. They are not two names for the same thing. A being can have deep magical knowledge and zero Universal Energy capacity, or immense Universal Energy and no magical training whatsoever.

Their relevance to combat follows an inverse relationship determined by realm level:

| Realm Level | Universal Energy | Magic | Baseline Abilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Highest | Dominant -- DBZ-level power | Irrelevant for combat | Irrelevant |
| High | Strong | Minor supplementary role | Minor |
| Mid | Moderate | Primary combat tool | Important |
| Low | Weak | Difficult to cast | Critical |
| Lowest | Near zero | Nearly impossible | Everything depends on this |

### Why Magic Becomes Irrelevant at High Levels

In high Universal Energy realms, the raw power available through UE so thoroughly outclasses anything magic can produce that casting spells in combat is pointless. A fireball means nothing to an opponent who can shatter mountains with their fist. Magic still *works* in these realms -- it simply does not matter against beings operating on a UE-enhanced scale.

Magic may retain utility for non-combat purposes in high realms: communication, healing, detection, manipulation of objects. But as a weapon, it is outclassed entirely.

### Why Magic Becomes Essential at Lower Levels

As Universal Energy drops, UE-fueled abilities diminish. The superhuman speed, durability, and destructive power that UE provides in higher realms fade. Combatants can no longer rely on raw energy to win fights.

Magic fills the gap. In mid-level realms, magic becomes the primary combat tool -- the difference between victory and defeat. Spells, techniques, magical constructs, enchanted weaponry -- all of these matter when nobody in the room can punch through a wall with UE alone.

This is where deep magical knowledge becomes the most valuable asset a fighter can have.

### Why Even Magic Fails at the Bottom

The lower a realm, the harder it is to do anything. Magic is not immune to this pressure. In the lowest realms, even casting becomes difficult or impossible. The ambient conditions suppress everything -- UE, magic, and eventually anything beyond baseline biology.

At the very bottom, a being is left with nothing but their body and their mind.

## The Protagonist's Magic

The protagonist carries approximately 60 subjective years of magical study from the Blaze Network (which she does not remember accessing). Her theoretical understanding and spell repertoire rival elves with decades of conventional training.

### Where Her Magic Peaks

Her magic is most valuable in **mid-level realms** -- the exact zone where magic is the primary combat tool and Universal Energy is not dominant enough to render it irrelevant.

The Swahili Realm (where the game takes place) sits at **mid-tier low** Universal Energy. This is within the zone where magic is her strongest asset. She can cast, she can fight with spells, and she can apply the full depth of her knowledge.

### How She Uses Magic in Combat

The protagonist's magical combat is not instinctive. She learned magic through study, not practice. The Blaze Network preserved knowledge but not physical muscle memory (it does not affect the body). This means:

- She draws on a vast library of studied techniques
- She selects and combines them with deliberation, not reflex
- Her magic supplements her primary weapon (pike) and secondary weapon (sword) rather than replacing physical combat
- She channels magic through and alongside her melee fighting -- enhancement spells, defensive wards, offensive bursts timed to weapon strikes
- Under pressure, she may hesitate as she reaches for half-remembered techniques that feel ancient rather than fresh

### The Anomaly Factor

The Swahili Realm is an Anomaly Realm. While magic functions semi-normally here, the anomalous physics may cause inconsistencies:

- Spells interacting with strange gravity in unexpected ways
- Effects propagating differently across planetoid boundaries
- Occasional unreliability in areas where the anomaly is particularly strong

This means even her deep knowledge cannot fully predict how her magic will behave. Adaptability matters as much as mastery.

## Open Questions

1. What schools or categories of magic exist in this world? Is it elemental, conceptual, runic, spoken, gestural?
2. How does elven magic specifically differ from other traditions? The protagonist was trained in the elven style -- what does that look like?
3. Can magic be stored in objects (enchantment)? If so, how does this interact with realm levels?
4. Is there a cost to casting -- stamina, mental fatigue, physical pain, material components?
5. How does magic interact with mecha suits? Can magic enhance or fuel a mecha?
