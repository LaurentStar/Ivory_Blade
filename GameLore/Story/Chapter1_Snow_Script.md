## Level 1: Crash into the Snow

---

### 1.1 -- Opening

```
[CUTSCENE]
(Black screen. Wind. The sound of impact -- metal tearing, something heavy hitting snow. Silence for a beat.)

(Cut to: the protagonist, face down in deep snow. 4'3". Alone. The Meta Morph Blade is half-buried a few feet away. Wind whips across the mountaintop.)

(Her hand moves. She touches her ear -- the earring is there. A faint warmth.)

INDIGO: ...Kim?

(Beat.)

KIM (from the earring, quiet, strained): I'm here.

(She exhales. Pushes herself up. Snow falls off her shoulders. She looks around -- nothing but white peaks, grey sky, and the distant shapes of other planetoids hanging in the void. No ship. No crew. Nothing familiar.)

(She pulls the Meta Morph Blade out of the snow. It shifts into pike form in her hands.)

(She stands. 4'3" against a frozen mountain. She starts walking.)
```

**Notes:** The opening should be quiet. No music sting, no dramatic reveal. She wakes up, checks the earring, gets up. That's the character in three actions.

---

### 1.2 -- Kim Check-in

```
[EARRING -- CONVERSATION]
KIM: Anything broken?
INDIGO: No. You?
KIM: You landed on your face. Your skull cushioned the blow for both of us. Thank you.
INDIGO: ...Shut up.
KIM: Hard head!
(Trigger: 30 seconds after the cutscene ends, if the player is not in combat.)
```

**Notes:** First earring conversation. Kim's humor is quick and dry -- he doesn't build to jokes, he drops them. "Make me. Oh wait." lands because he can't be made to do anything. He has no body. The joke is the situation. Indigo's "shut up" is affectionate, not annoyed.

## Level 2: Contact with Kat

---

### 2.1 -- Reaching High Ground

```
[GAMEPLAY DIALOGUE]
INDIGO: High enough. Open sightline. This'll work.
(Trigger: player reaches the designated signal point.)
```

```
[EARRING -- HINT]
KIM: If Kat's alive, she's listening. Send the signal.
(Trigger: player idles at the signal point for 10+ seconds without activating the magic signal.)
```

---

### 2.2 -- Establishing the Signal

```
[CUTSCENE]
(The protagonist kneels. She places her hands flat on the rock -- one palm glowing faintly with magic, the other adjusting a small tech device she pulled from her gear. She is building the signal with both disciplines simultaneously. Magic shapes the broadcast. Tech stabilizes the frequency.)

(A pulse of light leaves her position, arcing upward into the grey sky.)

(Static. Then:)

KAT (via magic signal, warm but focused): --got you. Indigo, I have your position. Are you hurt?

INDIGO: I'm fine. Where's the ship?

KAT: Intact. Ys and the mechanic are with me. We're grounded east. Making repairs.

INDIGO: Brod?

KAT: ...Not with us.

INDIGO: The mouse?

KAT: She's here. Shaken, not hurt.

INDIGO: So it's just Brod.

KAT: I'm scanning. Give me a minute.
```

**Notes:** This is the first time the player hears Kat. Her voice should be warm -- genuinely warm, not "professional calm." She cares. But she is also working. She is already scanning before Indigo asks about Brod.

---

### 2.3 -- Intel Dump

```
[COMMS]
KAT (via magic signal): Okay. Here's what I have. The soldiers on your mountain are a patrol unit -- Swahili East Axel Legion, low tier. Mixed squad. Huskies, humans, a few foxes. They're calling themselves a "Husky Squad" on their comms.

INDIGO: How many?

KAT: More than you've seen. They have positions across the mountain. Patrols, watch posts, at least one vehicle depot. They spotted the crash and they're converging on your area.

INDIGO: I've already been through three of their patrols.

KAT: I know. They know too. Their chatter just shifted from "investigate the crash" to "find the hostile."

INDIGO: Good.

KAT: That's not good, Indigo. That means they're organizing.
(Trigger: plays during gameplay after the cutscene. Player can move and fight while Kat talks.)
```

---

### 2.4 -- Brod's Capture

```
[COMMS]
KAT (via magic signal): Found him. Brod's signal is faint but it's there. He's on the mountain -- northeast face, lower elevation than you.

INDIGO: Is he moving?

KAT: No. And his signal is... wrong. It's there but it's not him initiating it. It's like his body is broadcasting but his mind isn't driving.

INDIGO: Mind control?

KAT: That's my read. The Husky Squad has interrogation tech. If they grabbed him after the crash and wanted to know who we are and what we're doing here...

INDIGO: They put a device on him.

KAT: Yes. He's probably hostile right now. If you approach him, he will fight you.

INDIGO: He will get styled on. I'll try not to look too good beating him....

KAT: Indigo.... please don't break him
(Trigger: continuation of the comms sequence.)
```

---

### 2.5 -- Kim on Brod

```
[EARRING -- CONVERSATION]

INDIGO: I heard her.
KIM: You know how to handle that, right?
INDIGO: Magic disruption. Precision cut. Don't damage the host.
KIM: Just making sure you weren't going to punch him through a mountain.
INDIGO: ...I wasn't going to punch him through a mountain.
KIM: That pause was not reassuring.
(Trigger: after the Brod intel comms end, during travel toward Brod's location.)
```

**Notes:** Light moment. Kim and Indigo's dynamic -- he checks, she deflects, neither of them is fully joking.

## Level 3: Controlled Tsumi

---

### 3.1 -- Finding Brod

```
[CUTSCENE]
(The protagonist crests a ridge and sees Brod below -- standing in an open snow clearing, his back to her. His posture is wrong. Rigid. Mechanical. His dual swords are drawn.)

(She can see the device -- a small piece of tech attached to the base of his skull, blinking faintly.)

(Brod turns. His eyes are unfocused. He raises his swords.)

INDIGO: Where's the magic book and the robot waifu? You taking me on your own?

(No response. He charges.)

INDIGO: Yup.

```

---

WARNING: Lethal attacks will kill the host. Use magic to break the mind control device.
```

```

(Trigger: fight begins.)
```

---

```

```

```
[EARRING -- HINT]
KIM: He's almost done. Magic, Indigo. Finish it clean.
(Trigger: Brod's health reaches the "finish" threshold.)
```

---

### 3.4 -- Sword of Light

```
[CUTSCENE]
(Brod staggers. He's beaten down but still standing -- the mind control won't let him stop. His swords are shaking in his hands.)

(The protagonist raises her hand. Light gathers -- not from the environment, from her. A blade of pure light forms in her palm.)

(She strikes. The sword of light hits Brod square in the chest. It is painful -- he screams -- but it does not cut. No blood. The light pulses through him and into the device on his neck. The device sparks, cracks, and falls off.)

(Brod collapses into the snow. Breathing hard. Eyes clearing.)

BROD (groggy, disoriented): ...What...

INDIGO: You're fine.

BROD: I feel like I got hit by a truck.

INDIGO: Magic truck. You're welcome.

(Brod looks at her. He does not say thank you. She does not expect him to.)
```

---

### 3.5 -- The Separation Argument

```
[COMMS]
KAT (via magic signal): Brod, your gear is being held at a husky field camp. Sketch and Tablet. I've located it -- small camp, four troops. Northeast, about two kilometers from your current position. Manageable.

BROD: ...Thanks. I'm going.

INDIGO: No. We need to move. Together.

BROD: I'm not leaving Sketch.

INDIGO: It's a book, Brod.

BROD: It's not a book.

INDIGO: We don't have time for --

KAT (via magic signal, calm but pointed): Indigo.

INDIGO: What?

KAT: The earring.

(Beat.)

KAT: If someone told you to leave it behind because it's just jewelry... would you?

(Silence. The protagonist looks at the earring. Touches it without thinking.)

INDIGO: ...Fine. Go.

BROD: (already moving)
```

**Notes:** Kat wins this argument in one line. She doesn't lecture -- she draws a parallel the protagonist can't argue with. This is Kat's intelligence: not just tactical, emotional.

```

## Script Notes

### Voice Direction -- IndigoLLiy
- Short sentences. She does not explain herself.
- Cocky in social situations, cold and focused in combat. The shift should be noticeable.
- She does not narrate her own abilities. She does not say "I'm strong" -- she throws a rock twice her size.
- When she is funny, she is dry. When she is serious, she stops talking entirely.

### Voice Direction -- Kim
- Calm. He is dying and he knows it. He has made peace with it in a way that occasionally cracks.
- He observes more than he advises. His hints feel like someone pointing something out, not giving orders.
- He is the only person who can make Indigo pause. He is the only person she listens to without pushback.
- His humor is gentle. Hers is sharp. They complement.

### Voice Direction -- Kat
- Warm but professional. She is working while she is talking. Her warmth is genuine but she never stops scanning, never stops calculating.
- She delivers bad news clearly and without softening it. She delivers emotional truths the same way.
- She is the smartest person in the room and she does not need to prove it. It just shows.

### Voice Direction -- Brod
- Few words. Flat delivery. He is not dramatic.
- "I'm going" and "It's not a book" are his most important lines in this chapter. Both are short, final, and do not invite discussion.

### Bark Volume
- Husky soldiers should sound organized but outmatched. They are professional but clearly not elite.
- The mech pilot should sound confident at first and increasingly panicked as the fight turns.
- Indigo's combat barks are minimal. Exertion grunts, short tactical observations. She is not chatty in a fight.
