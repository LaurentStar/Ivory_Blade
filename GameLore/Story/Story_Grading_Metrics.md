# Story Grading Metrics

> *A framework for evaluating the quality of Ivory Blade's narrative across every dimension that matters.*

---

## How to Use

Each metric is scored on a **1-5 scale**. Score the story honestly at each stage of development. A 3 is acceptable. A 4 is strong. A 5 means it could not be meaningfully improved without changing the story's identity.

---

## Character Metrics

| # | Metric | What It Measures | Questions to Ask | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Motivation Clarity** | Whether every character's reason for acting is immediately understandable | Can you explain why this character does what they do in one sentence? Does the audience ever have to guess? | /5 |
| C2 | **Motivation Depth** | Whether motivations have layers beyond the surface reason | Is the stated reason the real reason? Are there unconscious drives, contradictions, or costs the character doesn't acknowledge? | /5 |
| C3 | **Internal Consistency** | Whether characters behave in ways that match who they have been established to be | Does a character ever act out of character purely to serve the plot? Would this person actually make this choice given everything we know? | /5 |
| C4 | **Growth Arc** | Whether characters change in response to events, and whether that change feels earned | Is the character at the end different from the character at the start? Can you trace exactly which events caused the shift? | /5 |
| C5 | **Distinct Voice** | Whether each character sounds and feels like a different person | Could you identify who is speaking with the name removed? Do characters have verbal patterns, humor styles, or speech rhythms that are theirs alone? | /5 |
| C6 | **Flaws and Limits** | Whether characters have real weaknesses that cost them something | Do flaws actually affect outcomes, or are they cosmetic? Does the character ever fail because of who they are, not just what they face? | /5 |
| C7 | **Relationship Dynamics** | Whether relationships between characters feel specific, evolving, and two-directional | Does the relationship change over the story? Does each person in the relationship affect the other? Are the dynamics distinct from every other pairing? | /5 |

## Plot Metrics

| # | Metric | What It Measures | Questions to Ask | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Cause and Effect** | Whether every major event is caused by a prior event or character decision, not coincidence | Can you trace a chain of causation from the inciting incident to the climax? Are there events that happen only because the plot needs them to? | /5 |
| P2 | **Stakes Clarity** | Whether the audience always knows what is at risk and what failure looks like | At any given moment, can the player articulate what the protagonist stands to lose? Are the stakes personal, not just abstract? | /5 |
| P3 | **Escalation** | Whether tension increases over the course of the story in a way that feels organic | Does each chapter raise the difficulty, the danger, or the emotional weight? Does the escalation feel like a natural consequence of prior events? | /5 |
| P4 | **Pacing** | Whether the story moves at the right speed -- neither rushing nor dragging | Are there stretches where nothing meaningful happens? Are there moments that deserved more time but were rushed? Do breather moments exist between high-intensity sequences? | /5 |
| P5 | **Surprise vs. Inevitability** | Whether plot turns are both unexpected and, in hindsight, the only thing that could have happened | Do twists feel earned by prior setup? Could a careful reader have predicted them without them being obvious? Do they recontextualize earlier scenes? | /5 |
| P6 | **Setup and Payoff** | Whether things established early in the story pay off later | Are there setups that never pay off (dangling threads)? Are there payoffs that were never set up (deus ex machina)? Is the ratio balanced? | /5 |
| P7 | **Resolution Satisfaction** | Whether the ending resolves what it needs to and leaves open what it should | Does the climax address the central conflict? Are lingering questions intentional hooks or accidental loose ends? Does the ending feel earned by everything that came before? | /5 |

## Worldbuilding Metrics

| # | Metric | What It Measures | Questions to Ask | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | **Internal Logic** | Whether the world's rules are consistent and respected | Does the story ever break its own rules for convenience? If magic works a certain way in Chapter 1, does it still work that way in Chapter 4? | /5 |
| W2 | **Lived-In Quality** | Whether the world feels like it exists beyond the protagonist's story | Do places have histories? Do NPCs have lives that don't revolve around the player? Does the world feel like it was here before the story started and will continue after? | /5 |
| W3 | **Integration with Plot** | Whether the world's unique properties actively shape the story rather than serving as backdrop | Would this story be fundamentally different in a different setting? Do the world's rules create problems and opportunities that drive the plot? | /5 |
| W4 | **Clarity of Rules** | Whether the audience understands how the world works without needing a textbook | Can a player grasp the key rules (UE, magic, realm physics) through gameplay and story alone? Is exposition delivered through action, not lectures? | /5 |
| W5 | **Cultural Texture** | Whether societies, factions, and groups feel distinct and internally coherent | Do the two armies feel like different organizations with different values? Do elven customs feel genuinely alien rather than "humans with pointy ears"? | /5 |

## Thematic Metrics

| # | Metric | What It Measures | Questions to Ask | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Thematic Clarity** | Whether the story has identifiable themes that the audience can feel, even if they can't name them | If someone asked "what is this story about, underneath the plot?" could you answer clearly? | /5 |
| T2 | **Theme Through Action** | Whether themes are expressed through character choices and plot events rather than dialogue or narration | Do characters demonstrate the theme by what they do, not by what they say about it? Is the theme shown, never preached? | /5 |
| T3 | **Thematic Consistency** | Whether the themes are reinforced across multiple characters and plotlines, not just the protagonist's | Do secondary characters reflect, contrast, or challenge the same themes? Does the world itself embody the theme? | /5 |
| T4 | **Complexity Over Simplicity** | Whether the story resists reducing its themes to simple moral lessons | Does the story acknowledge that its themes have no clean answers? Are opposing viewpoints given real weight? | /5 |

---

## Emotional Metrics

| # | Metric | What It Measures | Questions to Ask | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Emotional Range** | Whether the story hits different emotional registers rather than staying on one note | Are there moments of humor, tension, sadness, triumph, dread, and warmth? Does the story earn each emotional shift? | /5 |
| E2 | **Earned Moments** | Whether the big emotional beats are set up by prior investment, not manufactured in the moment | When a character dies, sacrifices, or breaks down -- has the story invested enough in that character for the audience to feel it? | /5 |
| E3 | **Quiet Power** | Whether the story can be emotionally effective in small, subtle moments -- not just spectacle | Are there lines, glances, or silences that hit harder than any explosion? Does the story trust the audience to feel without being told to feel? | /5 |
| E4 | **Player Investment** | Whether the player cares about what happens next -- not just what happens, but to whom | If you removed the gameplay, would the player still want to know how the story ends? Do they care about these people? | /5 |

## Game-Specific Narrative Metrics

| # | Metric | What It Measures | Questions to Ask | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Ludonarrative Harmony** | Whether gameplay and story reinforce each other rather than contradict | Does what the player does in gameplay match who the protagonist is in cutscenes? Do mechanics express character (e.g., the earring hint system reflecting IndigoLLiy's bond with Kim)? | /5 |
| G2 | **Environmental Storytelling** | Whether the world communicates story through level design, props, and spatial arrangement -- not just dialogue | Can the player learn something about the world or characters by looking at a room? Do environments tell stories the script doesn't? | /5 |
| G3 | **Pacing Across Levels** | Whether narrative beats are distributed well across gameplay segments | Does every level have a narrative purpose, or do some exist purely as filler? Are story beats spaced to reward progression without overwhelming? | /5 |
| G4 | **Hint System Integration** | Whether Kim's role as hint system feels like a character interaction, not a game mechanic bolted on | Does Kim's personality come through in hints? Does the hint system ever serve double duty as character development? Does it feel like talking to a friend or reading a tooltip? | /5 |
| G5 | **Boss Fight Narrative Weight** | Whether boss encounters are story moments as much as gameplay challenges | Does each boss fight resolve or escalate a narrative thread? Does the fight's design reflect the relationship between the combatants? | /5 |
| G6 | **Cutscene-to-Gameplay Ratio** | Whether the story respects the player's time and agency | Are cutscenes earning their length? Could any story beat be delivered through gameplay instead? Is the player ever watching when they should be playing? | /5 |

## Dialogue Metrics

| # | Metric | What It Measures | Questions to Ask | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Subtext** | Whether characters communicate meaning beneath the literal words | Do characters ever say exactly what they mean, or is the real message between the lines? Is there a gap between what is said and what is felt? | /5 |
| D2 | **Economy** | Whether dialogue accomplishes its purpose without excess | Does every line do at least one job (advance plot, reveal character, build tension, deliver information)? Could any line be cut without losing anything? | /5 |
| D3 | **Natural Flow** | Whether conversations sound like people actually talking, not reciting exposition | Do characters interrupt, trail off, change the subject, or misunderstand each other? Does dialogue have rhythm? | /5 |
| D4 | **Character-Specific Language** | Whether word choice, sentence structure, and tone vary by character | Does IndigoLLiy sound different from Brod? Does Ys sound different from Kat? Could you tell who is speaking from the syntax alone? | /5 |

## Score Interpretation

| Range | Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 150-185 | **Exceptional** | Publication/release-ready narrative. Minimal revision needed. |
| 120-149 | **Strong** | Core narrative works. Specific areas need targeted improvement. |
| 90-119 | **Functional** | Story serves its purpose but has clear weaknesses across multiple categories. |
| 60-89 | **Developing** | Foundation exists but significant structural or character work is needed. |
| Below 60 | **Early Draft** | Story is in concept stage. Major development required across most categories. |
