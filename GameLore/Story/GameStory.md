# Ivory Blade -- Game 1 Story

> *She does not want to be a prophecy child. She does not care about this war. She wants to save Kim. But the war cares about her.*

---

## Series Context

The protagonist's ultimate goal is to reach the center of the Mirralata System and restore Kim Tiernan's soul. That journey spans approximately 10 games. This first game covers one chapter of that larger arc.

She has **1 year** from the moment Kim's soul was captured in the earring. The vessel is failing. Every day counts.

## The Inciting Journey

The protagonist needs to reach the center of the Mirralata System. Direct travel is not viable -- too many active conflicts block conventional routes through the realms.

Captain Ys finds her. He is not human: pale white skin, white eyes with no pupils, a shape-shifting ghost-like entity whose true nature is unclear. He believes the protagonist is a prophecy child 

The protagonist does not believe in the prophecy. She does not care about it, But she needs passage through dangerous space, and Captain Ys has a ship and a crew. She accepts the escort.

Ys's crew:

| Member | Description |
|---|---|
| **Captain Ys** | Shape-shifting ghost-like entity. Pale white skin, pupil-less white eyes. Mysterious. Hired the crew. |
| **Kat Arlean Mori** | Alien with rabbit-like appearance (blue skin, white fur). Second-in-command. Ys's hidden partner. Strategist, medic, intel source. |
| **Brod Tsumi** | 19-year-old Black male mercenary. Drawing-casting magic via sentient sketchbook (Sketch). Not loyal -- needs the money. |
| **Robert Poole (Mechanic)** | Slender, lanky, tall pink-haired human woman. Keeps the ship and equipment operational. |
| **Vennessa (Mouse Woman)** | Short mouse-like anthropomorphic woman. Drawing-casting magic via brush pen. Stowaway. |

The crew is small. Six people total including the protagonist. Ys hired almost everyone -- their loyalty is to him first, not to her. She is a passenger with a purpose, not the leader.

---

## Arriving in the Swahili Realm

The crew arrives in the Swahili Realm.

It is an Anomaly Realm: a vast expanse of planetoids with strange, inconsistent gravity. There is no single ground, no single sky. The environment is a three-dimensional scattering of floating landmasses at various scales, with gravitational pulls that shift between and around them.

Universal Energy level: mid-tier low. Magic is functional and is the protagonist's primary combat tool. She can access a small amount of UE to supplement her abilities, but not enough to become superhuman.

A civil war is raging in this realm between the **Swahili East Axel Legion** and the **People Swahili Brigade and Electives**. The crew has landed in an active warzone.

---

## The Core Conflict -- The Hunt

Both armies -- the Legion and the Brigade -- believe the protagonist is the prophecy child, a figure who can end the civil war in decisive victory for whichever side claims her. Both armies want her.

The protagonist does not want to be claimed. She does not want to fight their war. She wants to save Kim and move on. But she is trapped in the Swahili Realm with two hostile forces hunting her, neither of which she can ignore.

Two armies fighting each other. Both simultaneously hunting the crew. The civil war does not pause because they are looking for her. She is a high-value target being pursued across an active warzone.

Her weapons: pike (primary), sword (secondary), magic (supplemental), hand cannon (last resort -- enough stopping power to halt a garbage truck at 90 mph). She can take a bullet and survive. She can deflect and parry bullets with her pike or sword.

Kim is in the earring. She taps it and talks to him. He talks back. The clock is running.

## Chapter 1: Snow (6 Levels)

The protagonist is thrown from the ship in a brutal attack and crashes onto a snowy mountaintop planetoid. She is separated from the crew. The **Husky Squad** -- a low-tier patrol unit of husky-like anthropomorphic soldiers -- is already in the area.

| Level | Event |
|---|---|
| Level 1 | Crash into snow. Fight through husky patrols. Alone. |
| Level 2 | Magic signal contact with Kat. Intel on Husky Squad and Brod's capture. |
| Level 3 | Fight mind-controlled Brod (magic tutorial). Free him. Split up. |
| Level 4 | Husky Squad intercepted the magic signal, reorganizes. Reports up the chain to Wolf Squad. |
| Level 5 | Wolf Squad is en route. Urgency to leave the mountain. |
| Level 6 | **Mech boss fight.** Defeats the husky pilot. Takes radio, mimics pilot's voice with magic, misdirects Wolf Squad. Escapes underground via a warm river fed by a lava lake. |

The mech is a Metal Gear Solid-style combat walker -- armored, missile-equipped, deployed after the Husky Squad's units stopped responding. The protagonist's physical strength is on display: her punches send soldiers and debris flying. She defeats the machine through agility, pike work, and raw power the mech was not designed to handle.

The chapter ends with the protagonist still separated from the crew, traveling underground toward ground level. **Chapter 2 begins underground.**

## Phase 1: The Wolf Squad

The Swahili East Axel Legion deploys the Wolf Squad -- five wolf anthropomorphic elite soldiers -- to hunt the crew. A small, focused hunter-killer unit led by Master Loneel Jacky ("Jack," age 32). Professional, disciplined, gun-equipped. They command lower-ranking human soldiers who report to them and carry out supporting missions.

The wolf squad is dangerous but beatable. They are elite by military standards, but the protagonist -- with her 60 years of magical study, her physical conditioning, and the combat instincts imprinted by Elidyr and hardened by the Black Rose -- can fight them.

The wolf squad hunts the crew across the planetoid system. Engagements are frequent. The protagonist learns the terrain, learns the wolves' tactics, and begins to push back.

### The Crew Is Captured

The wolf squad succeeds in capturing Captain Ys's entire crew. Everyone except the protagonist. She is alone in the Swahili Realm's anomalous terrain with no allies, no ship support, and an earring containing her dying friend.

She does not attempt a direct rescue. The numbers are against her. Instead, she does what she has always done: she finds the angle nobody else would look for.

### Jack -- 1v1

The protagonist tracks down and engages Jack in single combat. She beats him. This is a straight fight, skill against skill, and she wins. She captures him alive.

### The Bait

Rather than kill Jack, she uses him. She sets a trap using the captured leader as bait, exploiting the squad's loyalty to Jack and their operational protocols. The trap works. Her crew is freed.

The wolf squad is broken. Jack was beaten and used against them. Their mission has failed.

This is not a quiet event. An elite military unit dismantled by a small crew -- their captain captured and turned into a tool -- sends a signal up the chain of command. The army takes notice.

---

## Phase 2: The Tiger Unit

The army's response to the wolf squad's failure is not to send more wolves. It is to send something worse.

The Tiger Unit is dispatched. Tiger-like anthropomorphic creatures. If the wolves were elite soldiers, the tigers are what gets sent when elite soldiers aren't enough. They are many times more dangerous -- a qualitative leap, not a marginal upgrade.

### Phase 2a: First Encounter -- Near Death

The protagonist encounters the tiger captain for the first time.

She almost dies.

This is not a close fight she narrowly loses. The tiger captain is beyond her in every direct combat metric. Speed, power, durability, tactical awareness -- he outclasses her completely. She escapes with her life and nothing else. She is badly hurt. The confidence built from defeating the wolf squad is gone.

The new reality is clear: she cannot fight the tigers the way she fought the wolves. The rules have changed.

### Phase 2b: The Chase

The tiger team pursues the crew through the planetoid system. The game shifts from combat to survival. The protagonist is not fighting -- she is running.

The tigers are relentless. The crew uses the anomalous terrain, the strange gravity, the planetoid gaps, and every trick available to stay ahead. The protagonist's wall running, agile movement, and spatial awareness -- all products of the realm's physics and her training -- become survival tools rather than combat advantages.

Every moment spent running is a moment on Kim's clock. The earring is failing. She cannot afford to be chased forever, but she cannot afford to fight either.

### Phase 2c: Final Encounter -- The Crew Fight

The protagonist is forced into a final confrontation with the tiger captain inside the ancient structure. She does not go in expecting to win alone. She has her crew.

The fight is the first in the game where **every crew member contributes.** Indigo holds the line against the tiger captain directly -- the rematch from the beating he gave her earlier. She is buying time while the crew prepares behind her:

- **Brod and Vennessa** draw magic modifications onto the ship's cannon, augmenting the laser into something far deadlier
- **Ys** powers the ship like a battery, channeling his own energy into its systems
- **Robert** aims the cannon -- his first contribution to a kill in the entire game
- **Kat and Tablet** deploy nets to lock the tiger captain in place

Indigo **kicks the tiger captain into the air.** Kat and Tablet's nets snap him in place mid-air. Robert fires. The augmented, overcharged laser hits him dead center.

He does not die. He is burnt, broken, and finally beaten -- but alive. The tiger captain who dismissed Jack as dead weight, who beat Indigo bloody, who believed he needed no one, goes down to a crew of "weak" people working together. His fate beyond this point is an open thread.

The tiger captain will be back. He survived. He knows her face. This thread carries into future games.

---

## The Protagonist's Defining Pattern

This game establishes who the protagonist is through action, not exposition.

**Jack:** She beats him in a fair fight. Then she doesn't kill him -- she uses him as a tool to rescue her crew. Intelligence over violence.

**Tiger captain:** She cannot beat him in a fair fight. So she doesn't fight fair. She uses her crew -- every single member doing the one thing they are best at -- takes the beating, and finds the one angle that works. Persistence over power. Teamwork over individual strength.

Both victories come from the same core trait: she refuses to accept that a situation is unwinnable. She does not have the strongest magic, the best body, or the most experience. She has 60 years of studied knowledge that feels like faded memory, a muscular teenage frame built through real training, and the absolute refusal to quit that comes from spending her entire life as the worst student in the room.
