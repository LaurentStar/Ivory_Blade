# Chapter 2: Underground

> *The snow is behind her. The wolves are not.*

---

## Context

The protagonist escaped the snowy mountaintop at the end of Chapter 1 by traveling underground alongside a river that feeds into a lava lake. Near the mountain entrance, the water is still cold -- runoff from the snow above. It warms gradually as the tunnels descend toward the volcanic system below. By the time the caves exit at ground level, the water is hot. She misdirected the Wolf Squad with a radio deception -- mimicking the mech pilot's voice to send them to the wrong sector. That deception has a shelf life.

The crew is split across three locations:

- **Rendezvous point:** Captain Ys, Kat, and the mechanic are with the ship. It is ready for departure. Kat is coordinating with everyone via magic signals while repairs and adjustments are being made.
- **Underground (alone):** The protagonist is moving through the cave system toward the rendezvous.
- **Underground (separate):** Brod has recovered Sketch and Tablet from the husky camp and is taking his own route through the caves. The mouse woman is also somewhere in the cave system.

The Wolf Squad is the primary threat. They are elite, professional, gun-equipped, and they do not quit. The radio silence from the mech pilot tells them something went wrong. They are done trusting remote intel. They are coming in person.

---

## Level 1: The Underground Route

The protagonist is moving through the cave system alone. A river runs through the tunnels -- **cold at the start**, still carrying snowmelt from the mountain above. The environment shifts from frozen mountaintop to damp stone and cold water. Not warm yet. The volcanic heat is deeper, further along.

**At the rendezvous point**, Captain Ys and the mechanic are with the ship, handling repairs and adjustments. **Kat is alone.** She has positioned herself as the crew's nerve center -- feeding the protagonist navigation intel via magic signals, tracking Brod's progress, maintaining awareness of the military's movements above ground. She is the only one coordinating. She is the only one who sees the full picture. And she is exposed. Everyone is converging on her position: the protagonist from one direction, Brod and the mouse woman from another, and -- unknown to her at this point -- the Wolf Squad following the paintings from a third.

**Brod has recovered his gear.** He raided the small husky camp (four troops -- manageable), retrieved Sketch and Tablet, and is now taking his own route through the caves toward the rendezvous. Along the way, he **finds the mouse woman** in the cave system. She has been leaving **directional paintings** on the cave walls -- markings to help guide scattered crew members toward the rendezvous point. She is doing her part to bring everyone home.

The paintings work. They are clear, well-placed, and easy to follow for anyone who knows what to look for. The problem is that they are easy to follow for anyone, period.

Above ground, the **general army** is searching the mountains. The radio deception from Chapter 1 has expired -- the false coordinates led nowhere, and the mech pilot is not responding. Regular troops sweep the snow and find nothing. She is already underground.

**Jack** (Master Loneel Jacky, the Wolf Squad leader) gets the idea to check the underground network. The mountain has cave systems fed by rivers -- a logical escape route, and the surface search is turning up empty. The underground is dangerous -- unstable terrain, volcanic heat deeper in, limited visibility -- but Jack is not the type to avoid a dangerous option when the easy ones have failed. He takes his squad below.

The Wolf Squad enters the caves. **Nemo Crowns** (second in command, the squad's primary hunter) spots the **mouse woman's directional paintings** and recognizes them as a trail. The wolves follow it. The paintings lead them directly toward the crew's location in the cave network.

**Gameplay establishes:** New environment (caves, cold river water, stone). The water is cold here -- the warmth comes later. The rendezvous setup (Kat coordinating, ship being repaired). Brod's reunion with the mouse woman. The mouse woman's paintings as a double-edged sword -- helpful to the crew, fatal as a trail for the wolves.

---

## Level 2: Contact Underground

The Wolf Squad follows the mouse woman's directional paintings through the cave network. The trail leads toward the rendezvous point -- toward the hidden ship.

Kat detects the Wolf Squad's approach. She knows they cannot be allowed to find the escape ship. If the wolves reach it, the crew loses their only way out of the Swahili Realm. The ship must stay hidden.

Kat makes the call: everyone except the mechanic moves **deeper into the caves** to intercept the wolves before they get close. The mechanic stays with the ship. Everyone else goes.

Kat guides all parties -- Ys, Brod, the mouse woman, and the protagonist -- to a **midpoint** in the cave network where the wolves are approaching. She coordinates the convergence through magic signals, pulling scattered people toward one location. Ys, Brod (with Sketch and Tablet), and the mouse woman arrive and link up. **The protagonist is not here yet.** She is still catching up from the delay of fighting the mech on the mountaintop. She has been behind schedule since Chapter 1's final level.

The crew comes together underground without the protagonist. They encounter the Wolf Squad. A fight breaks out. The caves are tight, the terrain is uneven, the river -- warmer now than Level 1, noticeably so -- cuts through the middle of the passage. The crew fights well -- Kat's scythe and magic support, Brod's drawing-casting with Sketch restored, Tablet engaging as Brod's lethal protector, Ys doing whatever Ys does in a fight. But the Wolf Squad is elite, the full squad is here, and the protagonist is missing.

The crew is driven out of the cave system. They do not choose where they exit -- they are **pushed out** through a secondary opening, forced to retreat in a direction the wolves dictate. The fight is not a rout, but the crew is on the back foot and losing ground. The wolves are between them and the ship.

They emerge near a **temple surrounded by waterfalls**. Open ground. Water on multiple sides. A structure that provides some cover but no real escape route. The crew has nowhere left to run.

### The Protagonist's Arrival

The protagonist arrives at the midpoint too late. The crew is gone. What she finds is the **aftermath** -- broken rocks, gouged cave walls, scorched stone, scattered debris. The evidence of a serious fight. She missed it.

She follows the breadcrumbs. The damage trail leads through the caves toward the exit -- broken terrain, blast marks, the signs of a retreating fight. The trail leads her to the **underground temple surrounded by waterfalls**. By the time she gets there, the fight is already decided.

**Gameplay establishes:** Kat's tactical command -- she makes the decision to intercept, coordinates the convergence, keeps the ship hidden. The crew fighting without the protagonist. The protagonist's late arrival as a consequence of the mech fight delay -- she sees the aftermath, not the battle. Environmental storytelling through destruction.

---

## Level 3: The Ship and the Temple

This level splits between two events happening simultaneously: the protagonist reaching the ship, and the crew making their last stand at the temple.

### The Protagonist -- The Mechanic's Signal

While following the trail of destruction, the protagonist receives a **wonky magic signal**. It is rough, unstable, barely held together -- not Kat's clean work. It is the **mechanic**. She does not know magic well, but she scraped together enough to send a distress call.

The mechanic is alone at the ship. The caves around the rendezvous have **wild animals** -- creatures in the underground ecosystem that the crew's presence and the fighting have stirred up. The mechanic is terrified. She has enclosed herself inside the ship, sealed in, too scared to come out.

The protagonist stops following the trail of destruction. She turns and follows the **mouse woman's directional paintings** on the cave walls instead -- they still lead to the rendezvous. She reaches the ship.

She fends off the animals outside and gets to the mechanic. The mechanic is shaken but unharmed. The protagonist comforts her -- not softly, not with gentle words, but in her way. The mechanic is safe. The ship is intact.

The protagonist uses **Kat's surveillance equipment** left aboard the ship to locate the rest of the crew. She can see where they are and what is happening.

### The Crew -- Last Stand at the Temple

What the surveillance shows: the crew is at the **temple surrounded by waterfalls**, fighting the Wolf Squad and losing.

Captain Ys, Kat, Brod (with Sketch and Tablet), and the mouse woman are holding the temple. Waterfalls roar around them. The structure provides walls to fight from, but the Wolf Squad has numbers, guns, and discipline. The crew is outmatched in a sustained engagement.

Kat's magic provides support. Brod's drawings create obstacles and distractions. Tablet cuts through wolves with mechanical precision. Ys does whatever Ys does in a fight. But the wolves are too many, too coordinated, and too well-armed.

The crew is **defeated and captured by the Wolf Squad.**

The protagonist watches it happen through the surveillance. She is at the ship, with the mechanic, too far away to intervene.

**Gameplay establishes:** The mechanic as a vulnerable crew member who needs protection. The protagonist's practical compassion. Kat's surveillance equipment as a new tool. The crew's limitations without the protagonist -- they lose. The protagonist seeing the capture but being unable to stop it creates urgency for what comes next.

---

## Level 4: Lava Caves

The protagonist leaves the mechanic at the ship and heads underground toward the temple to rescue the captured crew. She is descending -- going deeper, going lower, moving toward the volcanic system at the heart of the planetoid.

**Volcanic activity hits.** The lava lake that feeds the underground river system is surging. Magma is rising through the lower tunnels. The caves are filling with hot lava from below.

This affects everyone simultaneously:

- **The captured crew and the Wolf Squad** are forced toward **higher ground**. The wolves are chasing the crew upward, away from the rising lava, but they are also fleeing it themselves. The capture is still in effect -- the crew is still prisoners -- but everyone is running from the same thing.
- **The mechanic** gets the ship ready and begins **flying it to a safer location** above the volcanic activity.
- **The protagonist** is at the **lowest level**, below everyone else. She is in the lava caves -- the deepest part of the underground system, where the river water is hot and the volcanic system is closest. The magma is rising toward her first.

### Bassual Grutie

In the lava caves, the protagonist encounters **Bassual Grutie** -- the Wolf Squad's youngest and smallest member. Petite by wolf standards, the weakest fighter on the team. But he is on this squad for a reason, and he fights like it.

Bassual serves as the **climactic boss fight** of Level 4. The lava is rising. The environment is collapsing. The protagonist fights him as the ground beneath them fills with magma -- a battle with a literal deadline. She defeats him.

### The Report

Bassual **reports his defeat** to Jack. He fought the target solo and lost.

This gets Jack very curious. He has already seen what the rest of the crew is capable of -- they fought at the temple and lost. They are captured. They are not the real problem. But this girl took down the Husky Squad, deceived his unit with a radio trick, and just beat Bassual in the lava caves while the ground was melting.

Jack makes his decision. His team can handle the captured crew on their own -- Nemo, Elpha, and Bleu have proven that. He is going after the protagonist **himself.** He does not delegate this. He heads down into the lava caves to hunt her.

He does not find her yet. She has already escaped upward, away from the rising magma. But he knows where she is heading. He will find her again.

**Gameplay establishes:** Lava cave environment -- the most dangerous terrain yet. Rising magma as a persistent environmental hazard during a boss fight. Bassual as a preview of Wolf Squad quality -- even their youngest, weakest member is a real fight. Jack's decision to hunt the protagonist personally, motivated by curiosity and respect for the threat she represents. Everyone is being forced upward.

---

## Level 5: Frozen Lake

Everyone has been forced to higher ground by the volcanic activity. The crew and the Wolf Squad emerge from the caves onto the surface -- a **frozen lake**. The outside air is much colder than the caves below. The transition is brutal: from superheated lava tunnels to ice.

### Jack Goes Back Down

Jack does not stay with his team on the surface. He turns around and goes **back down into the caves** with two objectives: kill the protagonist and recover Bassual. Nemo, Elpha, and Bleu can handle the crew without him -- they have proven that.

### The Protagonist -- Underground

Jack encounters the protagonist in the caves. They fight. This is **not a boss fight** -- it is a skirmish, fast and violent. The protagonist does not win. She cannot beat him in a straight fight. But she does not need to win. She needs to survive.

She **traps him** -- using the cave environment, the collapsing tunnels, the rising heat. She maneuvers him into a dead end or a blocked passage, forcing him to take **another exit** out of the cave system. He is not defeated. He is not injured. He is delayed. Separated from his squad, forced to surface somewhere else, and behind on time.

### The Crew -- On the Ice

On the frozen lake, the crew fights the Wolf Squad under **Nemo's command** -- three wolves without Jack. This battle is **much more manageable** -- the wolves are still elite, but without Jack the crew can hold. Elpha's rifle discipline keeps the crew pinned while Nemo coordinates the approach. They are fighting, not fleeing.

But manageable is not enough. The Wolf Squad is still the Wolf Squad. They push the crew back, pin them down, and begin **capturing them one by one**. Physically handled, tied up. The fight is over.

The wolves consider **killing them on site**. The crew is more trouble than they are worth. These prisoners have already fought back twice, escaped once, and one of their number took down Bassual in the lava caves. Executing them is simpler than guarding them.

**Kat speaks up.** She tells the Wolf Squad that they have the **prophecy child**. The protagonist -- the one their leader is chasing right now -- is the prophecy child that both armies are searching for. This crew is her people. Killing them means losing leverage over the most important target in the Swahili Realm.

The wolves do not kill the crew. They **report it up the chain**. The information reaches higher command. The prophecy child is real, she is here, and the Wolf Squad has her crew.

**Gameplay establishes:** Frozen lake environment -- the temperature contrast from lava caves to ice. Jack's separation from his squad as a consequence of hunting the protagonist. The crew fighting without the protagonist and losing again. Kat's deception -- using the prophecy child claim to save the crew's lives. The stakes escalating from military to political as higher command gets involved.

---

## Level 6: Jack Falls

### Observation

The protagonist surfaces and sees the captured crew from a distance. They are tied up, guarded by the Wolf Squad on the frozen lake. The wolves are waiting -- higher command has been notified about the prophecy child claim. Nobody is being executed. For now.

She does not charge in. She decides to **retreat to the ship** and plan a proper escape.

### Finding the Mechanic

Using magic, the protagonist tries to locate the mechanic. She cannot actually contact her -- the mechanic is nearly magicless, so there is no signal to establish a two-way link. But the protagonist can sense her location: the mechanic has flown the ship to the **top of a giant tower**, high above the frozen terrain.

The protagonist heads for the tower.

### Jack

Midway to the tower, **Jack** finds her.

He surfaced from a different cave exit after being trapped in Level 5. He went back for Bassual. **Bassual is dead** -- killed by the lava, by the caves, by the injuries the protagonist inflicted. He did not make it out.

Jack has the full context now. Nemo reported that Kat claimed they have the **prophecy child**. Higher command has been notified. And the girl who is supposedly the prophecy child killed Bassual in the lava caves. The only piece missing is her. He wants to **capture her alone**. No squad, no backup. Just him.

### The Boss Fight

A serious fight. The environment is brutal -- **wind, snow, and lightning**. A **giant white tornado** churns in the background, dominating the horizon. The frozen surface is slick, visibility drops in and out, and lightning strikes the terrain unpredictably. This is not the tight caves of Level 4. This is open, exposed, and violent -- with the tornado pulling at everything, bending the storm around itself.

Jack is the best fighter the protagonist has faced so far. He has fought her once already (Level 5) and she only survived by trapping him. This time there are no caves to collapse, no tunnels to block. It is her against him on an open field in a storm.

This time is different. The protagonist has fought him once and learned how he moves, how he attacks, how he thinks. She is still smaller, still less experienced in raw military combat, but she adapts faster than he expects.

The fight is brutal. He pushes her hard. But the protagonist finds her angles. She uses the storm, the terrain, her agility, and the same refusal to lose that has defined every fight she has been in.

**The protagonist defeats Jack.**

He is alive. Beaten, but alive. She does not kill him -- she captures him.

### Chapter 2 Ends

The protagonist reaches the tower and the ship with the captured Jack. She has the mechanic. She has a hostage. The crew is still captured by Nemo, Elpha, and Bleu on the frozen lake.

**Chapter 2 ends here.** Jack is captured. The crew is not yet freed. What she does with this leverage -- how she uses the captured Jack to rescue the crew -- is the beginning of Chapter 3.

**Gameplay establishes:** The protagonist beating Jack one-on-one in a storm. Open terrain boss fight -- wind, snow, lightning, giant white tornado. Her tactical mind -- capturing instead of killing. The chapter ends on a cliffhanger: she has the piece she needs, but the crew is still in enemy hands.
