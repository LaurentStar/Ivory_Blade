## What It Is

The Mayler Realm is a realm known across the Mirralata System for one thing: **rare creatures end up here, and they do not leave in good condition.**

This is a realm where the acquisition, display, and trade of exotic and rare animals is not just tolerated -- it is the culture. Poaching is an industry. Animal abuse is a norm rather than an exception. Collectors compete for the rarest specimens. The conditions captive creatures endure are, by any standard, terrible.

The Mayler Realm is not a lawless wasteland. It is worse than that. It is a place with infrastructure, wealth, and social order -- all built around the exploitation of living things. The cruelty is not chaos. It is commerce.

---

## The Collector Culture

### Wealthy Collectors

The Mayler Realm's upper class measures status by the rarity of their collections. The rarer the creature, the higher the prestige. These are not conservationists or researchers. They are **trophy owners** -- people who see living creatures as displays of wealth and power.

At least one such collector -- a wealthy man who hoards rare and exotic pets -- took **Vennessa's star cat** after she refused his offers to buy it. He saw the cat, decided it was a rare breed worth owning, and simply took it when money failed. This is the kind of person the Mayler Realm produces: someone who believes anything rare belongs to whoever has the resources to claim it.

### Poaching Networks

The demand for rare creatures fuels organized poaching operations that extend beyond the Mayler Realm. Poachers travel to other realms, capture rare species, and transport them back to collectors willing to pay. This means the Mayler Realm's impact is not contained within its borders -- its appetite for rare creatures reaches across the Mirralata System.

### Conditions

The creatures kept in the Mayler Realm are not well cared for. The emphasis is on acquisition and display, not welfare. Cages, enclosures, and holding facilities exist to keep creatures contained and visible, not comfortable. The overall conditions for captive animals are bad -- neglect, confinement, and mistreatment are standard rather than exceptional.

## Why the Crew Goes Here

### The Rare Creature

In a future game, the crew's route toward the center of the Mirralata System is blocked by an **Anomaly Realm** they cannot cross with their current capabilities. They need a **rare creature** that can help them navigate or survive the crossing.

A **wealthy friend of Ys** -- a resident of the Mayler Realm -- pointed them toward the solution. A collector in the Mayler Realm has such a creature. The friend assured Ys that the caretaker **wouldn't miss or even notice** the creature was gone. The collection is so vast and the neglect so thorough that one creature disappearing would barely register.

Ys decided to **steal the creature and give it a better home**. This was never going to be a purchase or a negotiation. The crew is going to take a creature from someone who does not care for it, use it to cross the Anomaly Realm, and give it a life better than a trophy case. The heist is practical. The rescue is moral. Ys can be both.

### Ys's Wealthy Friend

The friend who tipped Ys off is a **resident of the Mayler Realm** -- someone wealthy enough to live there and connected enough to know which collectors have what. The nature of this friendship, how Ys knows someone in a realm built on animal exploitation, and what the friend gets out of helping are open questions. But the friend's tip is what sets the Mayler Realm arc in motion.

### Vennessa's Mission

The crew's strategic need and Vennessa's personal mission converge in the Mayler Realm. While the crew steals the creature they need, Vennessa has her own target: the **wealthy collector who took her star cat**.

Whether these are the same collector -- whether the man who has Vennessa's cat also has the creature the crew needs -- is an open question. Either way, the crew is operating in a realm full of people who hoard rare creatures, and they need to take something from one of them. Vennessa's knowledge of the collector, her personal stake, and her determination make her essential to the operation in a way she has not been before.

This is the arc where Vennessa stops being the passenger Ys plans to drop off and becomes a crew member with irreplaceable value.

## Narrative Potential

The Mayler Realm offers a fundamentally different kind of challenge than the Swahili Realm:

- **Not a warzone.** The danger is not armies and military hunters. It is wealth, corruption, security systems, and people who protect their collections with resources rather than soldiers.
- **Stealth and deception.** The crew cannot fight their way through a realm full of wealthy collectors. They need to infiltrate, deceive, and steal. Vennessa's drawing-casting magic -- which creates things that fool people -- becomes the crew's best tool.
- **Moral weight.** The crew is surrounded by suffering creatures. They are here for one specific creature (and one specific cat), but the scale of what they see raises questions about what they do with the opportunity. Do they take only what they came for? Do they do more?
- **Vennessa's character arc.** This is where her quiet determination meets the reality of what was done to her cat. This is where the normal, helpful mouse woman who paints directions on cave walls becomes someone with a personal enemy and a personal reckoning.

## Open Questions

1. **Realm type and UE level** -- Is the Mayler Realm a Standard Realm or an Anomaly Realm? What is its Universal Energy level?
2. **The rare creature** -- What does the crew need? What species? What does it do that helps them cross an Anomaly Realm?
3. **The collector** -- Who is the man who took Vennessa's star cat? Name, appearance, power, security. Is he a minor figure or a major antagonist?
4. **Is it the same collector?** -- Does the man who has Vennessa's cat also have the creature the crew needs, or are these separate targets in the same realm?
5. **Governance** -- Is the poaching and collecting legal in the Mayler Realm, or is it a black market? Is there a government that enables it, or is it simply unregulated?
6. **The crew's approach** -- How do they plan the heist? Who leads? Does the whole crew enter, or does a smaller team infiltrate while others stay with the ship?
7. **Which game?** -- Where in the 10-game series does the Mayler Realm arc fall?
8. **Vennessa's cat** -- What condition is the star cat in when they find it? Has it been mistreated?
9. **Consequences** -- Does stealing from the Mayler Realm's collectors create enemies that follow the crew into future games?
10. **Ys's wealthy friend** -- Who is this person? How does Ys know a resident of the Mayler Realm? What is their relationship? Do they appear in the game?
