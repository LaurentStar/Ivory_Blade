# The Dragon Fortress and the Ancient Seal

> *One is in the sky and everyone knows about it. One is in the ground and nobody did.*

---

## Why This Document Exists

These are **two separate locations** on the same planetoid, and they are routinely collapsed into one on the page. Chapter 3 takes place across both; Chapter 4 takes place inside one of them. Every scene that touches the dragon, the seal, or the armies depends on the distinction holding.

The two most common errors are recorded at the bottom of this document. Check here before writing either site.

---

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Site A** | The floating military fortress -- in the sky |
| **Site B** | The ancient structure -- in the ground, south of the fortress |
| **Relationship** | Separate places, far enough apart that a dragon has to fly the distance |
| **Chapters** | Ch3 uses both. Ch4 takes place entirely inside Site B. |

---

## Site A -- The Floating Military Fortress

An abandoned military installation that floats in the sky, visible from the ground only as a shape in the storm clouds.

**Who built it and who left.** A conventional military structure. The army evacuated it **recently -- within living memory** -- and for exactly one reason: a dragon nested in it. The installation was not worth the fight. This has nothing to do with the ancient sealing centuries earlier, and the two events must never be listed together as though they were.

**Layout.** The underside is industrial -- maintenance hatches, scaffolding, exposed structure -- which is the approach route. The interior is broken military architecture overgrown with coiling redwood-like trees that grew around and through the building. The dragon nests at the top, in a nest built from torn metal, uprooted tree wood, and debris from the fortress itself.

**Story role.** Chapter 3 Levels 1 through 3. Indigo climbs it from the underside in a storm, crosses the interior, reaches the nest, and steals the dragon's egg to lure the dragon south.

---

## Site B -- The Ancient Structure

An ancient sealed structure buried in the ground, **south** of the fortress. Its gate sits beneath the surface.

**What it looks like from outside.** Nothing. That is the point. Before the gate was blown open, there was no visible structure -- only ground. The natives of the realm did not know it existed. Nothing in Kat's records mentioned it.

**What it looks like from inside.** Not ancient at all. Shiny, metallic, advanced. Giant moving architecture that shifts and rearranges like the mechanisms of a colossal lock. It is older than the war and older than the armies.

**Story role.** Chapter 3 Levels 4 through 6, and the whole of Chapter 4.

### The Legion Camp

The **Swahili East Axel Legion** camped on this site. They pitched tents and parked vehicles directly on top of the buried gate and used the structure itself as cover from the hurricane, with no idea what they were standing on. They held the captured crew here.

They **built nothing**. There is no separate shelter building, no third structure, no Legion-constructed anything. The thing sheltering them was the thing they were camped on.

The **People Swahili Brigade and Electives never camped here.** A spy inside the Legion leaked the prophecy child's location, and the Brigade arrived **during the dragon assault**, turning a two-way fight into a three-way war. Both armies are present by the time the gate opens. Only the Legion had been living on top of it.

### How the Gate Opened

The dragon followed its stolen egg south from the fortress, hit the camp, and was driven past territorial rage into fury by the battle. It fired a white fireball straight down. The blast happened to strike the buried gate in exactly the right way to unseal it.

**Nobody planned this.** Not the dragon, not the armies, not the crew. The dragon had no relationship to the seal, never guarded it, was never drawn to it, and had never been near this site until it was lured here. The archive inside the structure documents centuries of history and has no entry for the dragon at all.

## Common Errors

Three mistakes have made it into finished pages. All three were caught only after they had been written, because this document did not exist.

1. **Inventing a third structure.** Writing the Legion as having "built a shelter" on top of the gate. They camped. Nothing was built.
2. **Two armies camped on the site.** Only the Legion. The Brigade arrives mid-battle and never occupies the site.
3. **Dating the fortress's abandonment to the ancient sealing.** The fortress was abandoned recently, over the dragon. The sealing was centuries ago. Listing them in the same sentence implies a connection that does not exist.

A fourth to watch for: **the dragon as guardian or deterrent.** It was neither. Its involvement is pure accident, and the records do not mention it.
