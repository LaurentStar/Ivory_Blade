## What It Is

The Mirralata System is not a galaxy. Not a solar system. Not a dimension. It is the **entire structure of reality** -- a system composed of **realms**, each of which is a self-contained universe arranged around a center.

Every realm is a **bubble containing an entire universe**. Not a pocket dimension. Not a room. A complete reality with its own space, environments, inhabitants, civilizations, and rules. Realms are separated from one another -- you cannot walk from one to the next. Crossing requires a vessel.

The system has a center. The protagonist's journey across 10 games is to reach it.

---



## The Three Pillars

Every realm in the Mirralata System is governed by three fundamental forces. Their values and behaviors vary from realm to realm, creating radically different conditions in each.


| Pillar               | Function                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Universal Energy** | The raw fuel of existence. Determines the ceiling for how powerful a being can become. Ranges from near-zero (baseline biology only) to extreme (Dragon Ball-level power). |
| **Magic**            | A separate force from UE. Inversely relevant to UE level -- dominant in mid-level realms, irrelevant in the highest, nearly impossible in the lowest.                      |
| **Physics**          | The fundamental rules of reality. Standard realms follow internally consistent physics (even if different from other realms). Anomaly Realms break their own rules.        |


The interplay of these three pillars makes every realm a unique environment. A mid-UE standard realm with conventional physics is a completely different experience from a low-UE Anomaly Realm with broken gravity. Beings who travel between realms must adapt or die.

## Realm Types



### Standard Realms

Standard realms maintain internally consistent rules for all three pillars. Their physics, while potentially different from other realms, can be studied, learned, and predicted. A being who spends time in a standard realm can build a reliable understanding of how it works.

### Anomaly Realms

Anomaly Realms operate on impossible logic. Their physics may be internally inconsistent, contradictory, or broken. Gravity shifts without warning. Space contradicts itself. Cause and effect decouple. These realms cannot be fully mastered because they do not follow their own rules consistently.

Full details: `GameLore/World_Physics/Realms.md`

## Travel



### Spaceships

The primary method of cross-realm transit. Physical vessels that cross the boundaries between realm-bubbles and deliver passengers into the next reality. Captain Ys's crew travels aboard one such ship.

### Realm-Traveling Trains

Public cross-realm transit infrastructure. Trains that carry ordinary passengers between realms on established routes. Robert Poole was riding one to visit family in Vitoko when her route was disrupted.

The existence of public transit implies **infrastructure** -- maintained routes, stations, schedules, civilian traffic. The Mirralata System is not a wilderness of isolated bubbles. It is a connected system with commerce, travel, and ordinary people going about ordinary lives between the realms that are trying to kill the protagonist.

### The Apotheosis Track

A specific route or pathway leading toward the system's center, accessible through a special door. Captain Ys navigates the crew past surrounding conflicts to reach the Mirralata System's door to the Apotheosis Track. This door deposits them into the Swahili Realm.

Whether the Apotheosis Track is the only route to the center, one of many routes, or a specific corridor that passes through particular realms in sequence remains to be determined.

### Travel Disruptions

The Mirralata System is not always safe to traverse. Large-scale events can block or destabilize entire regions of travel:

- **The Worm Guardian** -- A galaxy-sized worm guardian currently passing through part of the system, chasing bank robbers who disrupted realm-train tracks. The worm will take **1.2 years** to fully pass. Its presence **heavily disturbs** surrounding travel routes -- realm gates destabilize, navigation becomes unpredictable, and the dangers of cross-realm travel multiply. The official recommendation is to not travel until it passes.
- **Active wars** -- Military conflicts within realms create no-fly zones, patrols, and restricted areas.
- **Anomaly Realms** -- Navigating through realms with broken physics is inherently dangerous regardless of external conditions.

## Cross-Realm Power Dynamics

The Mirralata System punishes beings who cross too far from their native energy level. Travel between realms is not just logistically dangerous -- it is biologically dangerous.


| Direction                                   | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Descending** (high UE to low UE)          | Beings lose access to the energy sustaining them. The most powerful and UE-dependent tear apart -- their cells, organs, and structure fail without ambient energy. The strongest beings in the system may be prisoners of their own realm.           |
| **Ascending** (low UE to high UE)           | Beings gain disproportionate power. Surviving in low-energy environments builds baseline toughness. When that toughness meets abundant energy, the result is massive enhancement.                                                                    |
| **The Light Threshold** (ascending too far) | If the gap is too extreme, the being dissolves into light -- omnipresent, godlike, but without form. Functionally death, though calm rather than violent. Exceptionally composed individuals can maintain consciousness and retreat to reconstitute. |


This creates asymmetric danger:

- **Descend too far:** tear apart (violent, structural failure)
- **Ascend too far:** become light (calm, dissolution)

Both are fatal without extraordinary circumstances.

## The Center

The center of the Mirralata System is the destination that drives the entire series.

### What the Protagonist Believes

IndigoLLiy believes something at the center can **restore Kim Tiernan's soul**. Whether this belief comes from a rumor, a prophecy, something she was told, or something she discovered has not been fully established. She has one year before the earring containing Kim's soul fails. If she does not reach the center in time, Kim is obliterated permanently.

### What the Prophecy Says

A chosen child will overcome all challenges, travel to the center of the Mirralata System, and activate a **special event**. The prophecy does not clearly specify what this event is. Both armies in the Swahili Realm believe the protagonist is this chosen child. She is not -- the real chosen child is a separate, unknown individual.

### What Actually Happens

The protagonist's journey through the realms inadvertently **delays the real chosen child** by leaving behind harder, more prepared enemies. A rabbit god engineered this -- selecting the protagonist because her selfishness and survival instincts create chaos that slows the prophecy without stopping it. After the prophecy is fulfilled and a new order is established, the protagonist is positioned to **defy** it.

Full details: `GameLore/World_Physics/Prophecy.md`

## Cosmic-Scale Entities

The Mirralata System contains beings and entities that operate at scales beyond any individual realm.

### Worm Guardians

Galaxy-sized worm entities that function as guardians within the system. At least one is currently active -- chasing bank robbers through the system and blocking travel for 1.2 years simply by passing through. The worm's scale is so vast that its transit disrupts realm gates and navigation across surrounding regions.

The existence of worm guardians implies that the Mirralata System has **protective mechanisms** or **fauna** operating at cosmic scale. Whether the worms are natural creatures, engineered guardians, or something else is unknown.

### The Rabbit God

A divine or god-tier entity that happens to be a rabbit. The architect behind the protagonist's manipulation. It selected IndigoLLiy to slow the real chosen child and defy whatever order the prophecy establishes. Not to be confused with Kat Arlean Mori (the rabbit girl on Ys's crew), who is an alien, not a god.

Full details: `GameLore/World_Physics/Prophecy.md`

## What the System Implies

The Mirralata System is not a collection of game levels. It is a living, structured cosmos:

- **Public infrastructure exists.** Trains run between realms. People commute. Robert Poole was visiting family. There are ordinary lives happening across this system that have nothing to do with prophecies or wars.
- **Scale varies wildly.** Galaxy-sized worms share the system with 4'3" teenagers carrying earrings. The range of what exists here -- from cosmic fauna to civilian transit -- is enormous.
- **The center matters.** Everything in the narrative converges on the center: the protagonist's mission, the prophecy, the rabbit god's plan. Whatever is at the center of the Mirralata System is the fulcrum of the entire series.
- **Travel is the story.** The 10-game arc is structured around the journey through realm after realm, each with different physics, different UE levels, different civilizations, and different dangers. The Mirralata System is not a backdrop -- it is the structure that generates the narrative.

## Open Questions

1. **Realm arrangement** -- Are realms arranged in concentric layers (outer to inner), a branching tree, a web, or something else? Does "moving toward the center" follow a linear path or require navigating a complex structure?
2. **How many realms exist?** Dozens? Hundreds? Infinite? Does anyone have a complete map?
3. **What is at the center?** The highest Universal Energy? The lowest? Something that transcends the three-pillar system entirely?
4. **Realm boundaries** -- What exists between realms? Void? Dimensional barriers? Is the space between realms traversable, or do ships jump directly from one bubble to the next?
5. **The Apotheosis Track** -- Is it a physical road, a dimensional corridor, a series of connected realm doors? Who built it? Is it maintained, abandoned, or contested?
6. **Can realms be destroyed?** What happens to a realm's inhabitants if the bubble collapses?
7. **Inter-realm governance** -- Is there any political structure spanning multiple realms, or is every realm its own sovereign entity?
8. **The worm guardians** -- Are there more than one? What are they guarding? Who or what created them? Are they sentient?
9. **Vitoko Realm** -- What does this capital realm look like? What makes it a capital? Capital of what -- a political union, a trade hub, a cultural center?
10. **The protagonist's home realm** -- Name, UE level, and character. What is the elf-controlled nation like?
