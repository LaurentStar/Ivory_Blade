## Appearance

Elpha is physically imposing -- roughly the same size as Jack, making her one of the two biggest wolves on the team. Wolf anthro features with a build that communicates raw stopping power. She is the largest female in the squad and one of the largest overall. She carries her assault rifle like an extension of her body.

---

## Personality

Professional and direct. Elpha does not grandstand, showboat, or make speeches. She puts rounds where they need to go and trusts the rest of the squad to do their jobs. She is the squad's most reliable member in the simplest sense -- she does exactly what is needed, every time, without drama.

There is no recorded moment where Elpha hesitates, complains, or deviates from the plan. That consistency is her defining trait. In a squad of personalities -- Jack's frontline intensity, Nemo's cold pragmatism, Bassual's youth -- Elpha is the steady center. She does not need to be interesting. She needs to be dependable.

---

## Combat Role

Elpha is the reason the Wolf Squad can pin down an entire crew. Her combat function is zone control through sustained assault rifle fire.

**What she does:**
- During the cave fight (Ch2 L2), her fire keeps the crew from advancing, flanking, or retreating on their own terms
- During the frozen lake engagement (Ch2 L5), she pins the crew down while Nemo coordinates the approach and capture
- She does not chase targets. She creates kill zones and forces the enemy to move where the squad wants them

**What makes her dangerous:**
In the Swahili Realm's mid-tier low UE, the protagonist can survive a bullet -- but Elpha does not fire one bullet. She fires sustained bursts that force even the protagonist to deflect, dodge, or reposition. Her value is not in killing power per individual round but in battlefield control. She turns open terrain into a shooting gallery with defined safe zones and death zones, and the squad operates in the safe zones while the enemy runs through the death zones.

## Story Beats

### Ch2 L2: Cave Fight
Elpha's fire drives the crew out of the cave system toward the temple. She controls the engagement's pace -- the crew cannot hold position against sustained rifle discipline in tight tunnels.

### Ch2 L5: Frozen Lake
Under Nemo's command, Elpha pins the crew on the ice while the remaining wolves close in. Her sustained fire is the primary reason the crew cannot break free or mount a counterattack. The crew is captured one by one.

### Ch3 L2: Tiger Unit Arrival
Elpha's status during the Tiger Unit's arrival and Jack's freeing is not detailed. She is alive and present with the remaining squad.
