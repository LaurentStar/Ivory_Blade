## Appearance

Jack is the second largest member of the Wolf Squad -- slightly smaller than Elpha Merlissa but built with more concentrated power. Wolf anthro features: humanoid frame, lupine face, fur, digitigrade stance. He carries himself like someone who has never been surprised by a fight.

---

## Personality

Jack leads from the front. He does not delegate the hardest fights, and he does not ask his squad to do anything he would not do first. He is professional, methodical, and relentless -- not cold, but controlled. He does not get emotional about the mission. He gets emotional about his people.

When Bassual reports his defeat in the lava caves and then dies before Jack can reach him, Jack does not rage or grieve publicly. He goes back down into collapsing caves filled with rising magma to recover the body. That is his response. Not words. Action.

He is not cruel. He is not sadistic. He hunts the protagonist because it is his assignment, and later because she killed the youngest member of his squad. Both motivations coexist without contradiction -- the professional and the personal run in parallel, and Jack does not seem to distinguish between them.

---

## Fighting Style

Jack fights like a professional who has never needed desperation. Fast, powerful, and tactically sharp. He reads opponents quickly, adjusts mid-fight, and does not overcommit to attacks that leave him exposed.

In the caves (Ch2 L5), when the protagonist traps him using collapsing tunnels, he does not panic. He takes another exit. When they fight on open ground in the storm (Ch2 L6), he pushes her harder than anyone has in the entire game up to that point. He is the best fighter the protagonist faces before the tiger captain.

The protagonist beats him not because she is stronger or faster, but because she fought him once and learned how he moves. She adapts faster than he expects. Jack's weakness is that his fighting style is built on being better than everyone -- when someone matches him through adaptation rather than raw ability, he does not have a second gear.

## Story Beats

### Ch2 L1-L3: The Hunt
Jack leads the Wolf Squad underground after the mouse woman's directional paintings reveal a trail. He commands the squad through the cave fight that drives the crew out to the temple, and oversees the crew's defeat and capture.

### Ch2 L4: Bassual's Fight
Bassual encounters the protagonist alone in the lava caves and loses. He reports his defeat to Jack. Jack's interest in the protagonist shifts from professional to personal.

### Ch2 L5: Underground Encounter
Jack goes back underground with two objectives: hunt the protagonist and recover Bassual. The protagonist cannot beat him in a straight fight -- she traps him using the collapsing cave environment, forcing him to surface elsewhere. He is delayed, not defeated. Bassual is already dead by the time Jack could have reached him.

### Ch2 L6: Boss Fight -- The Storm
Jack finds the protagonist midway to the giant tower. Boss fight in brutal conditions -- wind, snow, lightning, a giant white tornado. He pushes her hard. She finds her angles, uses the storm, the terrain, and the lesson from their first encounter. She defeats him and captures him alive.

### Ch3 L2: Freed by the Tiger Unit
The Tiger Unit arrives at the ship, finds Jack tied up and the mechanic alone. The tiger captain unties Jack and scolds him -- the Wolf Squad was beaten by a teenage girl, Bassual is dead, and their leader was captured. Jack is visibly upset but does not challenge the tiger captain. The hierarchy is clear.
