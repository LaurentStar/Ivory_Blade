## Appearance

Bassual is petite by wolf anthro standards -- the smallest member of the squad by a clear margin. Where Jack and Elpha are imposing and Nemo is functionally built, Bassual looks like he should not be on an elite squad. He is. That is the point.

---

## Personality

Bassual's personality is not extensively documented -- he dies in the same chapter he is introduced. What is known comes from how he fights and how the squad reacts to his death.

He fights like someone who belongs on the Wolf Squad. Not with Jack's composed dominance or Elpha's mechanical reliability, but with the intensity of someone who knows he is the weakest link and refuses to be the one who breaks. He encounters the protagonist alone in the lava caves and does not hesitate, does not call for backup, does not retreat. He fights.

He loses. But he fights.

---

## The Lava Caves (Ch2 L4)

Bassual encounters the protagonist alone in the deepest part of the underground system. The river water is hot. The volcanic system is close. Magma is rising.

He serves as the Level 4 boss fight -- a battle with a literal deadline as the ground fills with lava beneath them. He is skilled and dangerous despite being the squad's weakest fighter. The protagonist defeats him.

After the fight, Bassual reports his defeat to Jack. His injuries from the fight are severe. The lava caves are collapsing. The magma is rising toward him.

Jack goes back down into the caves to recover him (Ch2 L5). He does not make it in time.

**Bassual is dead** -- killed by the combination of the protagonist's damage and the rising lava before Jack could reach him.

---

## Impact

Bassual's death is the single event that makes the Wolf Squad's hunt personal.

Jack was already professionally interested in the protagonist. She had dismantled the Husky Squad, deceived his unit with a radio trick, and proven herself a high-value target worth hunting personally. All of that was professional. Bassual's death adds weight that professionalism cannot account for.

Jack went back for him. Into collapsing caves. Into rising magma. He did not make it in time, and he does not talk about it. But when he hunts the protagonist through the storm in Ch2 L6, the fight has something behind it that the earlier cave skirmish did not.

For the **player**, Bassual serves a structural purpose: he is the preview of Wolf Squad quality. The squad's youngest, smallest, weakest member is a legitimate boss fight -- a dangerous opponent who pushes the protagonist in hostile terrain with a ticking environmental clock. If this is the Wolf Squad's worst, the player understands what the best will be.
