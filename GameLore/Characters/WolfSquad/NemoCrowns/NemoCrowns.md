## Personality

Pragmatic above everything. Nemo does not have Jack's frontline presence or personal investment in individual fights. He cares about the mission completing, not about who delivers the final blow or how clean the victory looks.

He is not cold -- he is efficient. When the crew is captured on the frozen lake and the wolves consider killing them on site, Nemo is the one weighing the tactical math. Keeping prisoners in a warzone is a liability. These prisoners have already fought back twice, escaped once, and one of their number took down Bassual in the lava caves. Executing them is simpler than guarding them.

When Kat claims they have the prophecy child, Nemo does not argue or hesitate. The math changes. He reports it up the chain without emotion. The mission is bigger than five wolves on a frozen lake.

---

## Role in the Squad

Nemo is the operational brain. While Jack is the strongest fighter, Nemo is the one who tracks, finds, and corners targets. He is the hunter.

**Key contributions:**
- Recognized the mouse woman's directional paintings as a trail in the caves (Ch2 L1) and directed the squad to follow them
- Commanded the Wolf Squad on the frozen lake when Jack went back underground after Bassual (Ch2 L5)
- Oversaw the crew's capture, considered execution, and stood down when Kat's prophecy child claim changed the calculation
- Reported the prophecy child intelligence up the chain to higher command via Bleu

Nemo runs the squad whenever Jack is absent -- and Jack is absent a lot in Chapter 2, chasing the protagonist underground while Nemo manages the captured crew on the surface. The squad does not miss a beat under his command. He is not Jack, but the squad does not need two Jacks. It needs someone who keeps the operation moving when the leader is hunting.

---

## Combat

Nemo is a capable fighter but not the squad's primary combat asset. His strength is in positioning -- putting the squad where they need to be, cutting off escape routes, and creating the conditions for Elpha's firepower and Jack's close-range dominance to work. He fights when required but prefers to direct.

## Story Beats

### Ch2 L1: The Trail
Nemo spots the mouse woman's directional paintings in the cave system and recognizes them as a trail. He directs the squad to follow, leading them toward the crew's rendezvous point.

### Ch2 L2: Cave Fight
The squad intercepts the crew underground. Nemo coordinates alongside Jack, driving the crew out of the caves toward the temple.

### Ch2 L5: Command on the Ice
Jack goes back underground. Nemo takes command. Three wolves (Nemo, Elpha, Bleu) fight the crew on the frozen lake. Elpha's rifle discipline pins the crew down while Nemo coordinates the approach. The crew is captured one by one. Nemo considers execution. Kat speaks up -- they have the prophecy child. Nemo stands down and reports up the chain.
