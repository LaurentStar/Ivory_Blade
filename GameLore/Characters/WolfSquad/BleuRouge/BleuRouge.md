## Personality

Competent and understated. Bleu is the 4th strongest fighter in a five-person squad, which means he is not the muscle and knows it. He does not try to be Jack or Elpha. His value is in keeping the operation running -- communications, equipment, surveillance tech, and the infrastructure that lets five wolves hunt across a planetoid system.

Bleu is the kind of squad member who never gets the credit and never needs it. The squad's coordination, their ability to track targets across chaotic terrain, their communication with higher command -- all of that is Bleu. Without him, the Wolf Squad's tactical advantage collapses. They become five strong fighters with no shared intelligence. With him, they are a machine.

---

## Role in the Squad

Bleu is the Wolf Squad's technical specialist. He handles:

- **Communications** -- all transmissions to and from higher command, including the critical report of Kat's prophecy child claim
- **Intelligence processing** -- when the Husky Squad's intercepted magic signal report reaches the wolves, Bleu processes the intel and turns it into actionable tracking data
- **Equipment maintenance** -- keeping the squad's gear operational across hostile terrain (snow, caves, lava, ice)
- **Surveillance tech** -- the infrastructure that gives the squad awareness of the battlefield beyond line of sight

Bleu is the system that makes the hunters and fighters effective. He is not the one who finds the trail or kicks down the door. He is the one who tells the squad which trail to follow and which door matters.

---

## Combat

Bleu can fight. He is the 4th strongest member of an elite military squad, which means he is a dangerous combatant by any normal standard. But within the squad, he is not the one they send into a 1v1. His combat contributions are supportive -- covering fire, equipment deployment, maintaining squad coordination during engagements.

He fights when the situation demands it. He does not seek fights out.

## Story Beats

### Ch2 L1: Processing Intel
The Husky Squad's intercepted magic signal report reaches the wolves. Bleu processes the data and turns it into usable tracking information, helping Jack and Nemo plan the underground pursuit.

### Ch2 L5: Reporting the Prophecy Child
After Kat claims the crew has the prophecy child, Nemo makes the decision to stand down. Bleu handles the transmission up the chain to higher command. This single report changes the scope of the mission from a squad-level hunt to a political event.

### Ch3 L2: Tiger Unit Arrival
Bleu's status during the Tiger Unit's arrival is not detailed. He is alive and present with the remaining squad.
