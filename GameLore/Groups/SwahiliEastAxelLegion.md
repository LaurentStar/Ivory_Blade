## Description

The Swahili East Axel Legion is one of two armies locked in a civil war across the Swahili Realm. They are an organized military force with a clear command hierarchy, tiered combat units, intelligence networks, supply lines, and the infrastructure of a sustained war machine.

The Legion is the faction that hunts the protagonist across the entire game. Every military enemy she faces -- from the Husky Squad patrols on the snowy mountaintop to the Tiger Unit captain inside the ancient structure -- belongs to this army.

---

## Military Structure

The Legion is a large military force with **many small elite squads** operating across its territory. These squads vary in species composition -- some are entirely anthropomorphic, some are mixed, some include humans at elite levels. The Legion's strength is not concentrated in a few super-units; it is distributed across numerous specialized groups, each assigned to different regions and operations throughout the civil war.

The Tiger Unit is dangerous, but it is **not the Legion's most powerful unit**. There are squads within the Legion that surpass even the tigers. The protagonist does not encounter them in this game because the escalation she faces is geographic, not organizational -- the units deployed against her were the ones closest to the action, not the best the Legion has to offer.

### How the Escalation Actually Worked

The protagonist did not "climb the ladder" of the Legion's hierarchy. She crash-landed in a patrol zone, and the response escalated based on proximity and availability:

1. **Husky Squad** -- the low-tier patrol unit already stationed on the snowy mountaintop. They were there. She landed in their zone. Not a targeted deployment.
2. **Wolf Squad** -- the nearest elite unit available when the Husky Squad's reports flagged a serious threat. Dispatched because they were close and capable, not because they were specifically next in line.
3. **Tiger Unit** -- deployed after the Wolf Squad's failure AND the prophecy child revelation. The combination of a failed elite squad and a politically critical target justified pulling a more powerful unit. The tigers were the nearest apex-tier squad that could respond.

The escalation was driven by **proximity to the action** and the **prophecy child revelation**, not by a formal tiered response system where one tier automatically triggers the next.

### Elite Unit Composition

The Legion's elite squads are not species-restricted as a rule. The Wolf Squad happens to be all wolves. Other elite units include humans, mixed species, and combinations the protagonist has not encountered. The anthropomorphic-only squads are common but not universal.

### Human Soldiers

Humans serve at every level of the Legion -- from rank-and-file infantry to elite squad members. In the units the protagonist encounters, human soldiers fill support and garrison roles under anthro command (the Wolf Squad commands human infantry as expendable support). But this is a feature of those specific units, not a Legion-wide rule. Other squads have humans in elite combat positions.

## Units Encountered in This Game

The Legion has many squads. The protagonist only encounters three of them -- the ones that happened to be closest to the action.

### Husky Squad
Low-tier patrol unit. Mixed species (huskies, humans, foxes). Assigned to the snowy mountaintop planetoid. The protagonist's crash-landing put her in their patrol zone. They were already there -- not deployed in response to her.

Notable member: the unnamed mech pilot operating the **Leedsichthys-Stepper-U0v2**.

Full profile: `GameLore/Groups/HuskySquad.md`

### Wolf Squad
Five-member elite hunter-killer squad. All wolf anthros. Led by Master Loneel Jacky ("Jack," age 32). The nearest elite unit available when the Husky Squad flagged a serious threat.

Members: Jack (leader), Nemo Crowns (2nd in command), Elpha Merlissa (assault rifle specialist), Bleu Rouge (tech support), Bassual Grutie (junior, deceased).

Full profile: `GameLore/Groups/WolfSquad.md`

### Tiger Unit
Powerful combat unit. Tiger anthros. Deployed after the Wolf Squad's failure combined with the prophecy child revelation -- the political stakes justified pulling a stronger unit. The tiger captain is many times more dangerous than Jack, but the Tiger Unit is **not the Legion's strongest squad**. There are units above them. They were the nearest high-power unit that could respond to the situation.

Full profile: TBD -- `GameLore/Groups/TigerUnit.md`

### What the Protagonist Has Not Seen

The Legion fields many other elite squads across the Swahili Realm -- some with humans in elite combat roles, some with species the protagonist has not encountered, some more powerful than the Tiger Unit. The protagonist's experience of the Legion is a narrow slice of a much larger military force. She fought whoever was closest, not whoever was strongest.

## Role in the Story

### Chapter 1: Snow
The Husky Squad patrols the snowy mountaintop where the protagonist crash-lands. They capture Brod, use mind control for interrogation, and attempt to contain the situation. The protagonist dismantles them. Their reports escalate up the chain, triggering the Wolf Squad's deployment.

### Chapter 2: Underground
The Wolf Squad hunts the crew through the cave system and across the frozen lake. They capture the entire crew except the protagonist. Jack fights her twice -- she traps him once and beats him once, capturing him alive. Bassual dies in the lava caves.

### Chapter 3: Dragon Fortress
The Tiger Unit arrives in response to the Wolf Squad's failure. The tiger captain frees Jack and scolds him. The Legion deploys an airship, ground forces, and the Tiger Unit against the protagonist during the dragon chaos. A **spy within the Legion** alerts the opposing army (People Swahili Brigade and Electives) to the prophecy child's location, causing both armies to converge.

### Chapter 4: Ancient Seal
The tiger captain slips inside the ancient structure before the gate closes. He is the Legion's only presence inside the sealed structure for the rest of the game.

---

## The Prophecy

The Legion believes in the prophecy -- a figure who can end the civil war in decisive victory for whichever side claims them. After Kat claims the crew has the prophecy child (Ch2 L5), the Legion's interest escalates from a tactical hunt to a political priority. Higher command is notified. The prophecy child becomes the most important target in the war.

---

## The Spy

The Legion has a spy problem. During Ch3, a spy within their ranks alerts the People Swahili Brigade and Electives about the prophecy child's location. This causes the second army to converge on the shelter during the dragon assault, turning a two-way fight into a three-way war.
