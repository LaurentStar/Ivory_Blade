## Description

A small, independent crew assembled by Captain Ys. The crew was hired -- their loyalty is to Ys first, not to the protagonist. She is a passenger with a purpose, not the leader. Ys believes she is a prophecy child and offered to escort her through the Mirralata System. The protagonist accepted because she needs passage through dangerous space to save Kim Tiernan.

The crew is not military. They are not a squad. They are a collection of individuals Ys brought together, each with their own reasons for being here. What binds them is Ys's payroll and whatever trust has been built through shared survival.

### Robert Poole -- The Mechanic

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Robert Poole |
| **Species** | Human |
| **Appearance** | Tall, slender, lanky. Pink hair. |
| **Role** | Ship engineer. Repairs and maintains the vessel. |
| **Combat** | Non-combatant. Nearly magicless. Needs protection. |
| **Key Trait** | Terrified, underestimated, and more capable than anyone gives her credit for. |

Robert keeps the ship running. She was **riding a realm-traveling train to the Vitoko Realm** -- just visiting family -- when bank robbers disrupted the tracks. The robbers were being chased by a **galaxy-sized worm guardian** so massive it will take **1.2 years** to pass, blocking all travel along that route and heavily disturbing surrounding Mirralata System travel. Stranded, she ended up with the crew by pure circumstance. She has no stakes in the journey beyond not wanting to be stranded. The crew **plans to drop her off** along their rerouted path, but that keeps not happening because they keep almost dying.

She has been terrified through most of the journey -- cave animals, volcanic activity, the Tiger Unit grabbing her by the throat -- but she comes through when it matters. She fired the magically augmented laser that took down the tiger captain. She flew the ship to safety during the volcanic activity. She contributed her engineering mind to the puzzle room in Chapter 3. Brod considers her one of the only normal people on the crew.

### Vennessa -- The Mouse Woman

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Vennessa |
| **Species** | Mouse-like anthropomorphic woman |
| **Appearance** | Short. Mouse features -- rounded ears, small frame, soft proportions. |
| **Role** | Drawing-caster (superior technique to Brod). Robert Poole's bodyguard. Support and utility mage. |
| **Combat** | Drawing-casting magic -- similar skill to Brod, but her brush tool produces far more detailed results than his sword-pens. Her drawings look like paintings where his look like sketches. Specialty is deception -- fake soldiers that pass for real troops. |
| **Key Trait** | Quiet, capable, single-minded. Stowed away on the ship -- never asked, knew Ys would refuse. Ys is looking for a safe place to leave her. |

Vennessa is a **stowaway**. A wealthy collector in the Mayler Realm saw her star cat, wanted it, and took it after she refused to sell. The worm guardian's travel disruption blocked regular people from cross-realm transit -- but not the rich. The collector left with her cat. Vennessa was stuck. She never asked Ys for passage -- she knew he would refuse. She hid on the ship until it was too late to turn back.

In a **future game**, the crew must venture into the Mayler Realm to steal a rare creature that can help them cross an Anomaly Realm. Vennessa's personal mission and the crew's strategic need converge -- she becomes essential, not just a passenger.

She uses drawing-casting magic at a higher level than Brod -- her fake soldiers in Chapter 3 were convincing enough to blend with real army troops on an active battlefield. She drew directional paintings to guide the crew through the caves in Chapter 2, which accidentally became the trail the Wolf Squad followed. Brod considers her one of the only normal people on the crew.

## Ship Capabilities

- **Cross-realm travel** -- the ship is built for traveling between realms in the Mirralata System
- **Flight** -- capable of atmospheric and space flight, though limited in extreme weather (could not fly in the hurricane)
- **Weapons** -- ship-mounted lasers, augmented by Brod's drawing-cast symbols in Chapter 4
- **Autopilot** -- can be remotely activated by Ys
- **Three pilots** -- Ys (primary), Kat (competent), Brod (backup)
- **Mechanic** -- Robert Poole handles all repairs and maintenance
