## Description

A small, independent crew assembled by Captain Ys. The crew was hired -- their loyalty is to Ys first, not to the protagonist. She is a passenger with a purpose, not the leader. Ys believes she is a prophecy child and offered to escort her through the Mirralata System. The protagonist accepted because she needs passage through dangerous space to save Kim Tiernan.

The crew is not military. They are not a squad. They are a collection of individuals Ys brought together, each with their own reasons for being here. What binds them is Ys's payroll and whatever trust has been built through shared survival.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Species** | Unknown. Shape-shifting ghost-like entity. |
| **Appearance** | Humanoid. Pale white skin. Pupil-less white eyes. |

| **Combat** | Pike/staff with scythe, laser, and buzz-saw modes. Terrible gun aim. Sharp pilot. |

### Robert Poole -- The Mechanic

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Robert Poole |
| **Species** | [TBD] |
| **Appearance** | Slender, lanky, tall. Pink hair/skin. |
| **Role** | Ship engineer. Repairs and maintains the vessel. |
| **Combat** | Non-combatant. Needs protection. |
| **Key Trait** | Scared easily but intelligent. Nearly magicless. Capable of a rough distress signal at best. |

Robert keeps the ship running. She has been terrified through most of the journey -- cave animals, volcanic activity, the Tiger Unit grabbing her by the throat -- but she comes through when it matters. She fired the magically augmented laser that took down the tiger captain. She flew the ship to safety during the volcanic activity. Brod considers her one of the only normal people on the crew.

Ys protects her. He brought her aboard as a passenger and shields her from the worst of what the crew faces.

## Ship Capabilities

- **Cross-realm travel** -- the ship is built for traveling between realms in the Mirralata System
- **Flight** -- capable of atmospheric and space flight, though limited in extreme weather (could not fly in the hurricane)
- **Weapons** -- ship-mounted lasers, augmented by Brod's drawing-cast symbols in Chapter 4
- **Autopilot** -- can be remotely activated by Ys
- **Three pilots** -- Ys (primary), Kat (competent), Brod (backup)
- **Mechanic** -- Robert Poole handles all repairs and maintenance
