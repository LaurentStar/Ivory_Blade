## Description

The People Swahili Brigade and Electives is the second army in the Swahili Realm's civil war, opposing the Swahili East Axel Legion. While the Legion is the faction that directly hunts the protagonist through its tiered military units (Husky Squad, Wolf Squad, Tiger Unit), the Brigade operates as the other side of the war -- present throughout the realm, fighting the Legion, and eventually converging on the protagonist when they learn she is the prophecy child.

The Brigade's involvement in the protagonist's story is more limited than the Legion's. They do not deploy named units against her until Ch3 L4, when a spy within the Legion tips them off about the prophecy child's location. Their arrival transforms the shelter battle from a two-way fight into a three-way war.

---

## Role in the Story

### Chapters 1-2: Background Presence
The Brigade is fighting the Legion across the Swahili Realm throughout the game. Their war is the reason the realm is a warzone -- military zones, patrols, checkpoints, and supply lines exist because two armies are actively killing each other. The protagonist navigates through their conflict without directly engaging Brigade forces.

### Chapter 3, Level 4: Convergence
A spy within the Swahili East Axel Legion alerts the Brigade about the prophecy child's location at the army shelter. The Brigade converges on the shelter despite the dragon attack, the hurricane, and the ongoing Legion presence. The prophecy child is worth the risk.

Both armies are now fighting:
- Each other
- The crew
- The dragon

The Brigade arrives "from the other direction" -- a separate force closing in on the same target from a different vector.

### Chapter 3, Level 5: The Ancient Structure
When the dragon's fireball unseals the ancient structure, both armies go all hands on deck. The gate is open, the sealed secret of the Swahili Realm is exposed, and both factions want in. The crew enters the structure before the gate closes. Both armies are locked outside.

## The Prophecy

Like the Legion, the Brigade believes in the prophecy -- a figure who can end the civil war in decisive victory for whichever side claims them. The Brigade wants the protagonist for the same reason the Legion does: she is the key to winning the war.

Whether the Brigade's interpretation of the prophecy differs from the Legion's is an open question.

---

## Military Structure

The Brigade's military structure has not been established in detail. What is known:

- They are large enough to sustain a civil war against the Swahili East Axel Legion
- They have the logistics and intelligence capability to receive spy reports and mobilize forces to a specific location during a hurricane
- They have enough military power to converge on a position held by the Legion, a dragon, and a hurricane simultaneously
- Their name -- "Brigade and Electives" -- suggests a structure that may differ from the Legion's traditional military hierarchy
