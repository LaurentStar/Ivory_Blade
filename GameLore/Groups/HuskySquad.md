## Description

The Husky Squad is a patrol unit operating in the snowy mountain terrain of a planetoid in the Swahili Realm. "Husky" is the squad's name, not a species description -- the unit is a mix of husky-like anthropomorphic soldiers, humans, and fox-like anthropomorphic soldiers. The name comes from the squad's designation, not its composition.

They are not elite. They are not the army's best. They are a low-priority unit assigned to a cold, remote area. The protagonist encounters them not because the army has targeted her, but because she crash-landed in their patrol zone. They stumbled into an opportunity.

They are weaker than the Wolf Squad in every respect: training, equipment, tactical coordination, and individual combat ability. The protagonist, even alone and disoriented after a crash, handles them without being overwhelmed. They are dangerous enough to be a threat to an ordinary person. They are not dangerous enough to be a threat to her.

---

## Equipment and Tactics

- Standard military firearms and cold-weather gear
- Operate in squad-sized patrols across the mountain terrain
- **Mind control devices** -- the Husky Squad possesses technology that allows them to place captured individuals under mind control for the purpose of **interrogation**. They used this device on Brod Tsumi after capturing him, making him hostile and compliant
- Supported by **mech units** -- when husky patrols stop responding, nearby mechs are dispatched to investigate and support. The mech encounter at the end of Chapter 1 is a direct result of the protagonist wiping out enough husky units to trigger a response

## Notable Members

### The Mech Pilot (Chapter 1 Boss)

The only individually notable member of the Husky Squad. An unnamed pilot who operates the **Leedsichthys-Stepper-U0v2** -- a military-grade combat walker armed with missiles and designed for the snowy mountain terrain. He is dispatched to the mountaintop after the protagonist wipes out enough husky patrols that command notices the silence.

He is the Chapter 1 boss fight. The protagonist defeats the Leedsichthys-Stepper through physical strength (punching dents into armor, ripping at exposed components) and pike work, exploiting the anomalous gravity that makes the mech's footing unreliable. After his defeat, she takes control of his radio and communication equipment to mimic his voice and misdirect the incoming Wolf Squad.

He survives the fight. He is beaten, not killed.

### Everyone Else -- Support Grunts

The rest of the Husky Squad has no individually notable members. They are generic patrol soldiers -- armed, cold-adapted, and competent enough to be dangerous to an ordinary person, but not distinctive. They patrol in squads, set ambushes after intercepting the protagonist's magic signal (Level 4), and report up the chain of command. None of them stand out as individuals.

The grunt soldiers are killed during gameplay. They are side characters -- enemies the protagonist cuts through without hesitation. The mech pilot is the exception: he is defeated and left alive.

---

## Role in Chapter 1

The Husky Squad is the introductory enemy. They exist to establish the Swahili Realm as hostile territory and to give the protagonist something to fight through while she is separated from the crew.

Their capture of Brod Tsumi and use of mind control creates the setup for the magic tutorial (Level 3). Their elimination triggers the mech dispatch that serves as the chapter's climactic escalation.

After Chapter 1, the Husky Squad's relevance diminishes. They are the warm-up. The Wolf Squad is what comes next.
