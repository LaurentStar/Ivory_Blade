## Description

The Wolf Squad is a small, focused hunter-killer unit deployed by one of the Swahili Realm's warring armies. Unlike the Husky Squad (mixed species under a name), the Wolf Squad is composed entirely of wolf anthropomorphic soldiers. Five members. Professional, disciplined, and dangerous.

They are deployed after the protagonist draws the army's attention by defeating the Husky Squad. The army assigns the wolves specifically to hunt the protagonist and Captain Ys's crew. This is their mission.

---

## Composition and Equipment

- **5 members** -- a tight, elite squad. Every member is a wolf anthro.
- **Gun-equipped** -- firearms are standard. Unlike the protagonist who treats her hand cannon as a last resort, the wolf squad uses ranged weaponry as their primary loadout
- **Command lower-ranking human soldiers** -- regular human troops report to the wolf elites and are deployed as expendable support. The squad does not operate alone; they have infantry backing
- **Balancing two priorities** -- their army's war directives (the actual conflict) and capturing the protagonist (a high-value target). These priorities compete for their attention and resources, creating windows the protagonist can exploit

## Members

### Master Loneel Jacky -- "Jack" (Leader)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Squad Leader |
| **Nickname** | Jack |
| **Species** | Wolf anthro |
| **Gender** | Male |
| **Build** | Second biggest in the squad. Strongest member overall. |
| **Role** | Commander, frontline fighter |
| **Status** | Captured alive by the protagonist (Ch2 L6). Freed by the Tiger Unit (Ch3 L2). |

The Wolf Squad's leader and its most dangerous fighter. Jack is the strongest member of the squad despite not being the largest -- his combat ability is a tier above everyone else on his team.

**Fighting style:** Jack fights like a professional who has never needed desperation. He is fast, powerful, and tactically sharp. When the protagonist traps him in the collapsing caves (Ch2 L5), he does not panic -- he simply takes another exit. When they fight on open ground in the storm (Ch2 L6), he pushes her harder than anyone has in the entire game up to that point.

**Personality:** Jack leads from the front. He does not delegate the hardest fights. When the young wolf (Bassual) reports his defeat in the lava caves and then dies, Jack goes back down into the caves himself to recover him. When he learns the protagonist is the real threat, he hunts her personally. He is not emotional about any of this -- it is professional, methodical, and relentless. But he went back for Bassual's body, and that says something.

**Story beats:**
- Ch2 L5: Encounters the protagonist underground. She cannot beat him in a straight fight -- she traps him using the cave environment to force him to surface elsewhere.
- Ch2 L6: Boss fight. Open terrain -- wind, snow, lightning, a giant white tornado. The protagonist defeats him and captures him alive.
- Ch3 L2: The Tiger Unit arrives at the ship, threatens the mechanic, and frees Jack. His squad is broken. His role after this point is unresolved.

### Nemo Crowns -- Second in Command

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Second in Command |
| **Species** | Wolf anthro |
| **Gender** | Male |
| **Role** | Hunter, field operations lead |
| **Status** | Alive. Present during crew capture events. |

The squad's second in command and its primary hunter. While Jack is the strongest fighter, Nemo is the one who tracks, finds, and corners targets. He is the operational brain when Jack is absent -- and Jack is absent a lot in Chapter 2, chasing the protagonist underground while Nemo manages the squad and the captured crew on the surface.

**Role in the squad:** Nemo handles the hunt. He is the one who recognized the mouse woman's directional paintings as a trail and followed them underground (Ch2 L1). He is the one coordinating the squad when Jack goes back into the caves after Bassual. When Jack is trapped and forced to surface elsewhere, Nemo commands the wolves on the frozen lake -- overseeing the crew's capture, considering execution, and ultimately standing down when Kat claims they have the prophecy child.

**Personality:** Pragmatic. Nemo does not have Jack's frontline presence or personal investment in individual fights. He cares about the mission completing, not about who delivers the final blow. When the crew is captured and the wolves consider killing them on site, Nemo is the one weighing the tactical math -- not out of cruelty, but because keeping prisoners in a warzone is a liability. Kat's prophecy child claim changes his calculation, and he reports it up the chain without hesitation.

### Elpha Merlissa -- Assault Rifle Specialist

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Squad member |
| **Species** | Wolf anthro |
| **Gender** | Female |
| **Build** | Roughly the same size as Jack. One of the two largest members. |
| **Role** | Assault Rifle Specialist, heavy firepower |
| **Status** | Alive. Present during crew capture events. |

The squad's firepower anchor. Elpha is physically imposing -- roughly the same size as Jack, making her one of the two biggest wolves on the team. Where Jack fights up close and personal, Elpha controls engagements at range with assault rifle discipline.

**Role in the squad:** Elpha is the reason the Wolf Squad can pin down an entire crew. During the cave fight (Ch2 L2) and the frozen lake engagement (Ch2 L5), her sustained fire keeps the crew from advancing, flanking, or retreating on their own terms. She does not chase. She locks down zones and forces the enemy to move where the squad wants them to move.

**Personality:** Elpha is professional and direct. She does not grandstand or showboat -- she puts rounds where they need to go and trusts the rest of the squad to do their jobs. She is the squad's most reliable member in the sense that she does exactly what is needed, every time, without drama.

### Bleu Rouge -- Tech Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Squad member |
| **Species** | Wolf anthro |
| **Gender** | Male |
| **Build** | Average |
| **Role** | Tech support, communications, equipment |
| **Strength** | 4th strongest in the squad |
| **Status** | Alive. Present during crew capture events. |

The Wolf Squad's technical specialist. Bleu handles communications, equipment maintenance, surveillance tech, and the infrastructure that lets five wolves hunt across a planetoid system. He is the reason the squad stays coordinated across the Swahili Realm's chaotic terrain.

**Role in the squad:** When the Husky Squad's intercepted magic signal report reaches the wolves, Bleu is the one who processes the intel and turns it into actionable tracking data. When the squad needs to report up the chain to higher command -- including Kat's prophecy child claim -- Bleu handles the transmission. He is not the hunter or the fighter; he is the system that makes the hunters and fighters effective.

**Personality:** Bleu is competent and understated. He is the 4th strongest fighter in a five-person squad, which means he is not the muscle and knows it. His value is in keeping the operation running -- and in a squad this small, that value is enormous. Without Bleu, the wolves are five individuals in a warzone. With him, they are a coordinated unit with eyes, ears, and reach.

### Bassual Grutie -- The Young Wolf

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Squad member (junior) |
| **Species** | Wolf anthro |
| **Gender** | Male |
| **Build** | Petite. Smallest and weakest member of the squad. |
| **Role** | Junior member |
| **Status** | **Dead.** Defeated by the protagonist in the lava caves (Ch2 L4). Died from injuries and the rising lava before he could be recovered. |

The youngest and smallest member of the Wolf Squad. Bassual is petite by wolf standards -- the weakest fighter on the team. But he is still on this squad for a reason, and he fights like it.

**The lava caves (Ch2 L4):** Bassual encounters the protagonist alone in the deepest part of the underground system, where the river water is hot and the volcanic system is closest. He serves as the Level 4 boss fight -- a battle with a literal deadline as magma rises beneath them. He is skilled and dangerous. The protagonist defeats him.

**Death:** Bassual reports his defeat to Jack. He fought the target solo and lost. The lava caves are collapsing, the magma is rising, and his injuries from the fight are severe. Jack goes back down into the caves to recover him (Ch2 L5). He does not make it in time. Bassual is dead -- killed by the combination of the protagonist's damage and the rising lava.

**Impact:** Bassual's death is what makes the fight personal for Jack. The wolf leader was already professionally interested in the protagonist -- she had dismantled the Husky Squad and deceived his unit with a radio trick. But Bassual's death adds weight. Jack went back for him. He does not talk about it. But when he hunts the protagonist through the storm in Ch2 L6, the fight has something behind it that the earlier skirmish in the caves did not.

**For the player:** Bassual is the preview of Wolf Squad quality. Even the squad's youngest, smallest, weakest member is a real boss fight. If this is their worst, what is their best?

## What Makes Them Dangerous

- **Persistent.** Not a single encounter. A squad that hunts the crew across the planetoid system.
- **Organizationally supported.** Lower-ranking soldiers, intelligence networks, logistics. The crew is being hunted by a machine, not a handful of fighters.
- **Professionally motivated.** This is their assignment. They follow doctrine, they don't get emotional, they don't stop.
- **Dual-priority tension** creates opportunities -- moments when the wolves are pulled away to handle war obligations, giving the crew windows to move.

---

## Combat Dynamics

In the Swahili Realm's mid-tier low UE, the protagonist is durable enough to take a bullet. Standard gunfire hurts but does not instantly kill her. She can deflect and parry bullets with the Meta Morph Blade.

The wolf squad is dangerous but beatable. The protagonist -- with 60 years of magical study, physical conditioning, and combat instincts hardened by the Black Rose -- can fight them. They are elite by military standards. She is something outside their standard.

---

## Key Story Beats

**The Hunt:** The wolf squad hunts the crew across the planetoid system. Engagements are frequent. The protagonist learns their tactics and pushes back.

**The Crew Capture:** The wolf squad captures Captain Ys's entire crew except the protagonist. She is alone.

**Bassual's Fight (Ch2 L4):** The protagonist fights Bassual in the lava caves as the magma rises. She defeats him. He dies.

**Jack Goes Back (Ch2 L5):** Jack goes underground to recover Bassual and hunt the protagonist. She traps him in the caves. He surfaces elsewhere. Bassual is already dead.

**Jack 1v1 (Ch2 L6):** The protagonist tracks down Jack and beats him in single combat in a storm -- wind, snow, lightning, giant white tornado. She captures him alive.

**The Bait (Ch3):** She uses the captured Jack as bait to free her crew, exploiting the squad's loyalty to their leader and their operational protocols. The trap works. The crew is freed.

**The Signal:** An elite military unit dismantled, their captain captured and used against them. This sends a signal up the chain of command. The army's response: the Tiger Unit.
